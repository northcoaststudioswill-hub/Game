# Pocket Lantern

A fake handheld console for your phone or computer. Open `pocket-lantern.html` in Safari (or any browser) and it runs.

## Opening it

- **iPhone:** tap the file in Files and choose Open in Safari. To put it on your home screen: Share, then Add to Home Screen.
- **Computer:** double-click the file.
- Everything runs inside the file. No internet needed.

## Controls

- **D-pad:** move
- **A:** act, talk, throw a sprout (hold to aim)
- **B:** call your sprouts back
- **Select:** swap sprout type
- **Start:** pause

On a computer: arrow keys move, Z = A, X = B, Enter = Start, Shift = Select.

## Cartridges

- **Sprouts:** a cozy game. Gather sprouts, bring home sun shards, build your house and valley projects.
- **North Coast Knockout:** a two-player-style fighter. Buttons: A, B, X, Y.

## Saves

Progress saves in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data erases it. Keep the file, not the save.
