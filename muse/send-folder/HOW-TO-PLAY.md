# Pocket Lantern v0.3

A pocket handheld console for your phone or computer, with four cartridges. Open `pocket-lantern.html` in Safari (or any browser) and it runs.

## Opening it

- **iPhone:** tap the file in Files and choose Open in Safari. To put it on your home screen: Share, then Add to Home Screen.
- **Computer:** double-click the file.
- Everything runs inside the file. No internet needed.

## The cartridges

Pick one off the shelf. SWAP CARTRIDGE on each game's menu takes you back to the shelf.
Under the cartridges is the **Lantern menu**: one capsule machine for every game, stats, and Lantern achievements.

- **Sprouts:** the cozy one. Gather sprouts, bring home sun shards, build up your house and the valley.
- **North Coast Knockout:** a 2D fighter with four face buttons.
- **Rootdeep:** a roguelite under the Sprouts valley. Dive, fall, come back stronger, go again.
- **Patch & Plate (new):** Erin's farm stand. Grow it, cook it, serve it. Each day is a short level, about one to three minutes.

## Patch & Plate in one minute

- **The D-pad walks. A does whatever Erin is facing** (the bar under the stand always says what). **B** puts down (tosses) what she's holding.
  **Select** opens the recipe book. **Start** pauses.
- Customers walk up to the three windows with an order card above them. Take seeds from the crates, plant them, pick what grows,
  chop it, bake it, grill it, fry it or juice it, put it on a plate, and serve it at their window. Faster service means bigger tips.
- **Louie** naps by his bowl in the kitchen. When a bubble pops up, he wants kibble (the bag), a loaf (the tin) or fresh chopped lettuce.
  Sometimes two of them. Sometimes he changes his mind. Press A on him to pet him. A happy Louie means bigger tips.
- **Sprout seeds** come from new days and wait in the clay pot at the end of the garden. Plant one, pluck the sprout, and it helps
  in the kitchen from then on.
- Between days, at **Erin's stand**: PLAY starts the next day, and today's three little asks pay coins. Spend coins on upgrades
  and Erin's wardrobe; check your sprouts and friends (hearts grow as you serve people).
- **It saves on its own**, all the time. Put the phone down mid-day and the day pauses itself. There are three save files
  (SAVE FILES on the title or the stand) if more than one person plays on the same phone.

## Controls on a computer

Arrow keys move. Z = A, X = B, C = X, V = Y, Enter = Start, Shift or Q = Select.

## Good to know

- Saves live in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data erases them. Keep the file, not the save.
- The Sprouts title music streams from the original page, so a downloaded copy plays without it. Everything else, including
  Patch & Plate's song (Cook E), Knockout's and Rootdeep's music, is built in.
- This is a pre-alpha build (v0.3). Things will change.
