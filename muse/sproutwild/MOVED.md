# Sproutwild has moved

As of Nov 15 2026 (v6.0) Sproutwild is cartridge 5 of the main console. Its live source is **`alt/src/sproutwild/`**
and it is built by `python3 alt/build.py`.

This folder is kept as the archive of the standalone version (Sprouts + Rootdeep + Sproutwild, the shape Muse shipped):
lil Bottie's original files, the v5.0 to v6.0 source as it was at the moment of the move, the build scripts and tests.
Nothing here was deleted. Frozen playable copy: `muse/games/Sprouts+Rootdeep+Sproutwild-v6.0_2026-11-15_BACKUP.html`.

New work goes in `alt/src/sproutwild/`. Changes made there are not copied back here.
