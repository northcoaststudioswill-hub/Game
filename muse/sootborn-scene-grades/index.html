The bar

The baseline: B-

The new Keep film is a 42-shot 3D film timed to Kael's voiced narration, with figures that act out each line. It stops at B- because most of it has only been checked in stills, and nobody's face moves.

  • VoiceKael narrates the whole scene, and the picture keeps time with the voice.
  • ActingPoses shift and breathe, footwork is keyed, blows land with reactions, a sword's length apart.
  • CameraMoves in every shot: close-ups on faces, cut-in angles where a move would clip, wide then close.
  • MemoriesThe game's painted MEMORY panels cut in, plus a voiced 3D memory at the ford.
  • TextSerif subtitles over the picture, phrase by phrase.
  • SetA keep built for it, standing on its ridge in the valley.

Missing for higher: lip-sync and faces, a score written for the scene, every shot watched in motion, a phone test.

What to do

How to raise every scene

The fastest gain is reuse. All 14 of the game's films are on 3D sets, so once the Keep film's acting layer, voice clock, subtitles and memory cut-ins move into the engine, each could reach B- without new engine work.

  1. Apply the Wednesday fix list. It clears every rule clash on this page except the floating keep. Text only, cheap, first.
  2. Ground the keep. Use the preview's keep and ridge for every scene on the keep set (2, 20, 22, 31). Leave the Crown and the Stair on cloud, where the Spire is.
  3. Voice Kael. Record his narration in the same ElevenLabs voice, scene by scene. It lifts the films most and needs no art.
  4. Port the acting layer into the engine. It lives in the preview's ch1new.js; moved into g3.js and brawl.js, every film gets keyed poses, footwork, face zooms and cut-in angles.
  5. Swap the pixel captions for the new subtitles in every film, so they all read the same.
  6. Re-stage the still films. The Ford, the Standard, the Pairing, the Gallery, the Son and Riser Nine have one moving figure or none.
  7. Use the drawn panels as memory cut-ins inside the films, the way the Keep does, instead of as stand-alone slideshows.
  8. Fix the fallback films or cut them (Riser Nine, the High Docks, the Stair), as Will planned.
