# Pocket Lantern: story notes (Will, Nov 17)

## Defeat is permanent, with no penalty

- A darkened buddy that can't be shown the light **dies**. It doesn't stay in the grass.
- If you can't show a buddy the light, the dark stays and keeps the valley dark. So a defeat is sometimes the only way to clear it.
- **No penalty** for not showing a buddy the light. Nothing is taken from the player for it.
- Defeat is not the goal. Showing the light is still the main way to play.

This replaces the earlier v9.1 rule ("we cleanse, we do not knock out") and closes the question in REFINE-SLICE.md.

## The soul tribute (a later item)

- A tribute item collects the souls of buddies that were defeated and not shown the light.
- Its purpose: the souls can still be shown the light, in a way the story will explain later.
- The idea starts with **the keeper (Will's character)**. After **Aaron** tells him that the buddies died, he comes up with the idea of collecting their souls.
- It's a tribute to every buddy you didn't reach. It's not a punishment.
