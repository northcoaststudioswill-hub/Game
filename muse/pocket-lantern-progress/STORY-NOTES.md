# Pocket Lantern: story notes (Will, Nov 17)

## Defeat is permanent, with no penalty

- A darkened buddy that can't be shown the light **dies**. It doesn't stay in the grass.
- If you can't show a buddy the light, the dark stays and keeps the valley dark. So a defeat is sometimes the only way to clear it.
- **No penalty** for not showing a buddy the light. Nothing is taken from the player for it.
- Defeat is not the goal. Showing the light is still the main way to play.

This replaces the earlier v9.1 rule ("we cleanse, we do not knock out") and closes the question in REFINE-SLICE.md.

## The soul tribute, and King Bibi's whisper

- **Aaron is Erin.** Erin's name in the story. Erin is the one who tells the keeper the buddies died.
- **The characters appear from the beginning.** The soul idea doesn't appear late. It's set up early, so it can grow.
- **The scene: King Bibi "mind-dumps" HASBARA into the keeper's mind.** (HASBARA is Bibi's super in Knockout.) He puts the idea of the souls into the keeper's head, so the keeper thinks it's his own idea. The player shouldn't know this at first.
- **The device:** the keeper has a device that can capture the souls of defeated buddies. He doesn't know what to do with the souls yet.
- **The soul tribute:** the collected souls go into the tribute item (name still open). Later, the story says how the souls can still be shown the light.
- **Erin's explanation:** after the first defeat (see the tutorial notes in REFINE-SLICE.md).
- **Marcus's word, "J-lighting":** this is Marcus's own word, for the keeper taking Bibi's ideas and calling them his own. Later, when you go back to tell him about the souls, he says it.
- **Marcus's reaction:** he says the keeper is "J-lighting" again, creating false narratives, and too beholden to King Bibi.
