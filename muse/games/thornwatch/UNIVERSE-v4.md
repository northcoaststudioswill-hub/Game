# Thornwatch — how it fits the console (research for v4.0, Nov 17, 2026)

Source: the repo's own files as of Nov 17 (Pocket Lantern's Sproutwild source and changelog, the Rootdeep handoff, the Knockout source, LATEST.md). Anything below marked "not checked" was not read in the source.

## The console, as it stands

Five cartridges in one console (LATEST.md): **Sprouts** (the original, read-only), **North Coast Knockout** (a 2D fighter), **Rootdeep** (a roguelike hub), **Patch & Plate** (not expanded since v0.3), and **Sproutwild** (Pocket Lantern's creature game, the live project). Thornwatch is meant to be a sixth, standalone cartridge in the same universe.

## Shared world (taken from the source)

- **Places.** Sunkeep is the home base, with the Great Lantern at its centre. Lantern Town is the north road. Crooked Hollow is east of the Meadow, Squire's Woods to the south (an old castle with its gatehouse awake), the Ember Wastes, the Dewmist Shore, Gloom Hollow, and Zephyr Peak where the Data Center ruins are.
- **Cast.** Erin (the gardener; her warmth and her "Show them the light" line), Marcus ("bro", "see one, send one", "I supervise"), Louie (a Shiba Inu, the lookout, who barks warnings and fetches), Bottie (the robot who nuked the Data Center; "bzzt", "Oops in advance"), King Bibi (the scared king who raised the Iron Dome "temporarily"). Knockout's roster includes Erin, Marcus, Louie, King Bibi, Lil Bottie, Mama, Corbin and Derek.
- **Story.** The valley was promised to sprouts and buddies 3000 years ago, under the Great Lantern. Bottie nuked the Data Center, the simulation glitched, the Lantern cracked and went dark. The buddies went wild, and the biggest became alphas. Sprouts carry the light back to them. Thornwatch's bramble fits this without changing it: it is what grows in the dark when no sprout tends the soil.
- **Sprout types** are Sprouts canon (EMBER, PEB, DEW, ZIP, LEAF, LUCKY), and Thornwatch already uses them as its towers. Keep the same colours and order.
- **Sprite art** is ported from Sprouts and Rootdeep, as the handoff requires. Sproutwild's buddies (Bessling, Cuyapup, Grasskin/MOSSMAN, Melonkin, Fernfang and others) and its Shiba Louie are available as art.

## Easter egg hooks in the other games

- **Sproutwild:**
  - the Wending Lake monster BESSLING, a rare wild in the Long Beds
  - a castle stone as a tower skin (Squire's castle)
  - MOSSMAN, formerly Grasskin, as a sightseer or a hero skin
  - the ladybug, the valley's "luck bug", as a bonus sprite that rides a creep
  - the Lantern's "LIGHT SHOWN" count as Thornwatch's sun-drop economy
  - the Data Center's glitch as a "SIMULATION ERROR" screen on a secret night
- **Rootdeep:** the Hearth is a hub where you return between runs. Thornwatch's Long Night could use the same "return and spend" rhythm (not checked in the source).
- **Knockout:** Erin's "ERIN IS NOT HAVING IT." and Louie's moves are a fighter's callbacks. A Thornwatch cut-scene could borrow one line in the same voice (not checked for copyright or canon; ask Will first, per the handoff).
- **Sprouts (original):** the Louie "good boy" and berry lines are the origin of Louie's voice. Keep his lines short.

## Rules to keep it in the universe

0. **The fighting game feeds the other games, not the other way round.** Its characters and moves were used in the other games. Later, things can feed back into it.

1. **Canon names come from Will.** Move names are his fighting-game canon (handoff §5, rule 12). Ask before adding any.
2. **Standalone, but one world.** A cartridge can be played alone. A nod to another game should be a small surprise, never a requirement.
3. **Reuse is fine.** Bottie and Bibi can appear (Will, Nov 17). Only the placeholder characters (Kiss, Scout, Ranger, Elder and similar) are open to change, so don't build meaningful stories around them.
4. **Art is ported, not drawn new,** wherever a family sprite exists (handoff rule 12).
