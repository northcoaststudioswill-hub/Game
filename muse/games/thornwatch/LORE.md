# Thornwatch (working title) — lore for v4

Written Nov 18, 2026, after Will changed the enemy to lil Bottie's drone swarm. It is a proposal: everything here is Will's to change. Nothing in it changes Sproutwild's story.

## What the universe already says (not invented here)

All of this is from Sproutwild's own story (`alt/src/sproutwild/sw-lore.js`, `sw-light.js`) and Knockout:

- The valley was promised to sprouts and buddies 3000 years ago, under the Great Lantern.
- The DATA CENTER under the Peak is the machine that keeps the whole valley running.
- BOTTIE pressed the big red button and nuked it. The simulation glitched. The Great Lantern cracked and went dark.
- KING BIBI raised the IRON DOME, "temporarily", and shut the roads.
- The light fell as seeds, and the seeds grew legs: SPROUTS. Sunkeep still grows them. Erin is its gardener.
- Erin, on Bottie: "Now he wants the light for his feed."
- Bottie's tower is up the Peak road. The keeper faces him there at the end of Sproutwild.
- In Knockout, Bottie talks like a machine learning model (context window, rate limit) and is called a clanker.

## What Thornwatch adds

**Why Bottie needs the gardens.** He nuked the Data Center, and the Data Center was what fed him. Now he has nothing to run on. Erin's line is the whole motive: he wants the light for his feed. The valley's light is gone from the Lantern, but it is still in the ground wherever sprouts are grown. The brightest of it is the GARDEN HEART, the flower the first seed of light grew into, in Erin's back garden behind the cracked Lantern.

**What the drones are.** Bottie cannot leave his tower, so he builds drones out of the Data Center's wreck and sends them down the valley to bring the light back to him. He calls it "training". Each drone that reaches a Heart carries a piece of the light away. That is why the Heart has a number, and why it goes out at zero.

**Why sprouts can stop them.** Sprouts are light with legs. A drone is built to drink light slowly; a sprout gives it more than it can hold, all at once. That is every tower in the game: EMBER burns, ZIP overloads, DEW shorts them out. This matches Sproutwild's rule that sprouts carry the light, without using its battles.

**Why nobody else comes.** The Iron Dome shut the roads. It keeps people in. It does not keep drones out: they come over it and under it. King Bibi knows, and sends a letter. So the gardens are held by whoever is already there: Erin, Marcus, Will, and Louie on lookout.

**Why the swarm gets worse.** Every drone that gets home with light teaches the next batch. That is the difficulty curve, and it is why each garden has new drone types. Bottie says it himself: it is not stealing, it is learning.

**The three gardens.**
1. Erin's Garden, Sunkeep. The first Heart. Boss: THE HARVESTER, a machine built to mow a garden rather than sip from it.
2. The Long Beds, the rows that feed Sunkeep. Boss: THE SCRAPER, which swallows what is planted.
3. The Crossroads, old stone from before the Lantern, where two roads meet one Heart. Boss: BOTTIE PRIME, a bigger, madder copy of himself. In the game it is his own model, shown big.

**How it ends.** Prime goes dark and the drones drift home. Bottie goes quiet at the Peak, which is where Sproutwild's keeper finds him. So Thornwatch sits beside Sproutwild, during the same dark: the keeper is out winning Sigils while the people at home keep the light they still have. Neither game needs the other.

**The Long Night** (endless mode) is the nights that follow: he keeps sending them.

**Who Bottie is here (Will, Nov 18).** He looks like his normal self, he is mad, and he is the NUMBA ONE CLANKA. He says so in every scene he is in. The gardens keep breaking his drones and it makes him angrier each time.

## A new name

"Thornwatch" was named for the bramble and Old Thornback, and both are gone. The shelf is Sprouts, Rootdeep, Sproutwild: short compound words. Options, best first:

1. **SWARMWATCH.** Keeps "watch" (Louie is the lookout, and the old saves and file names barely change), and names what you are watching for.
2. **LIGHTKEEP.** Names what you protect; echoes Sunkeep. Softer, less about the fight.
3. **CLANKERFALL.** Funniest, and uses Knockout's word for Bottie. Reads more like a joke game.

**Chosen, Nov 18: CLANKAWATCH.** Will asked for something Bottie related. It keeps "watch" and uses the game's own word for him. Applied to the title screen and the cartridge label; the folder, file and save key keep the old name.
