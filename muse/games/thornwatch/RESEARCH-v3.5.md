## What makes BTD6 WIDE (and its money matter)

1. **Many maps, one roster.** BTD6 ships dozens of maps across
   Beginner→Expert. The towers never change; the TRACK does — a
   spiral makes pierce and corners rich, a long straight makes range
   rich, dual lanes split your money. Replay value comes from
   re-solving placement, not from more rounds on one board.
   → ADOPTED as the spine of v3.5: THREE GARDENS. Erin's Garden
   (the S-bend, 10 nights) + THE LONG BEDS (a long 3-row snake —
   straights are long, the middle row is covered twice) + THE
   CROSSROADS (two entrances converging on one Heart leg — money
   must defend two gates). 26 nights of length THROUGH width.
2. **The banana farm: a tower that is pure economy.** Farms cost
   real defense money, produce cash DURING rounds, and pay off only
   over time — every round is a greed-vs-safety bet, and the bet is
   what makes mid-game money decisions interesting instead of
   automatic.
   → ADOPTED: LEAF's SUNLEAF fork becomes SUNFARM — no attack,
   grows a visible petal fruit every few seconds during the night
   that banks itself (+float). It replaces SUNLEAF's Heart mend
   (flagged "possibly generous" since v2.0 — the farm role is the
   better second LEAF path; GROVE stays the dawn-economy fork).
3. **Tier-5 towers: money sinks with a face.** BTD6's tier-5s cost
   more than a whole early game; there is ALWAYS a next thing worth
   saving for, which is exactly the cure for "winning runs end
   2,000 petals rich" (our O1 flag since v3.4).
   → ADOPTED as GRAND forms: every fork can grow once more (Lv3)
   for 340–480 petals into a named GRAND (GRAND TESLA STORM,
   GRAND WILDFIRE HEART, ...). One per tower, dramatic multipliers,
   a gold-star tell. The playtest must prove a Grand converts the
   flood into power WITHOUT auto-winning.
4. **Per-map modes multiply the same content.** BTD6: Standard,
   Hard, Alternate Bloons Rounds, Impoppable, CHIMPS — same map,
   different rules and round orders, each with its own medal.
   → ADOPTED, light: per map, STANDARD → unlocks HARD (+35% creep
   HP, 40 starting petals, double Sun Drops per star) and ALT
   NIGHTS (the same nights remixed — late kinds arrive early,
   +bonus creeps). Stars are tracked per map × mode × night.
5. **Powers: money as an emergency verb.** BTD6 Powers are one-time
   consumables bought with game cash and fired mid-round — they
   give money a job at the exact moment a leak is happening, not
   just at dawn.
   → ADOPTED: a 3-power panel on SELECT during a round — SUNBURST
   (150p, heavy damage to everything on screen), RAIN OF DEW
   (80p, all creeps slowed hard 10 s), LOUIE'S HOWL (100p, stun
   all + reveal camo 8 s). SELECT was demoted in v3.3 for being
   awkward as a primary control; as an in-round "open the toolbox"
   key it earns its place back.
6. **Heroes level up.** BTD6 heroes gain XP and grow abilities;
   Monkey Money also buys hero progression. The emotional sink:
   you invest in a CHARACTER, not a stat.
   → ADOPTED in-run: HERO TRAINING — a placed hero can be trained
   Lv1→3 for petals (120 / 220), +35% damage per level, abilities
   faster and stronger, gold pips as the tell. Always available,
   always a real choice against one more tower.
7. **Hand-authored round variety.** BTD6 rounds are composed, not
   generated — camo rushes, regrow storms, ceramic spikes land as
   authored "events" the player learns.
   → ALREADY Thornwatch's way (composed nights + Louie's warning
   naming the threat); v3.5 keeps authored comps for all 26
   standard nights and writes ALT as authored remixes (bonus
   injections of later kinds), not RNG soup.
8. **Meta currency with a real store.** Monkey Knowledge / medals
   give cross-run goals beyond "win once."
   → ALREADY ADOPTED in the original v3.5 brief and kept: SUN
   DROPS (1 per first-time star; 2 in Hard; +1 per 5 endless
   nights, cap 4/run) buying 8 permanent LANTERN UPGRADES.

## Rejected, with reasons

- **BTD6's 100+ rounds per map / Impoppable / CHIMPS:** length
  again — Will explicitly wants width first. Our endless (THE
  LONG NIGHT, unlocks at Map 3's finale) carries the "how far can
  I go" itch instead.
- **XP-gated tower unlocks (new players can't use towers):**
  Thornwatch's strip is fully visible from night 1 and the guide
  teaches by doing; gating would fight the onboarding we just
  built. Progression lives in maps/modes/perks instead.
- **Insta-monkeys / cash drops / IAP-shaped consumables:** Powers
  here are petal-priced and earned in-run only. No second premium
  currency; Sun Drops are never purchasable, only earned.
- **Co-op / races / odysseys:** no multiplayer exists on the
  Lantern shell; out of scope honestly.
- **Per-map XP / hero XP carrying across runs:** hero TRAINING is
  in-run (a sink every run), not cross-run — cross-run power is
  the Lantern Upgrades' job, so the two systems don't blur.
