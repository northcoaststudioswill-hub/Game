Sootborn v0.84 · the script in the game, checked against itself and the story bible

Script audit

The story holds together. From the furnace to Row K, what happens in the game is what the story bible says should happen, and the big reveals are set up properly. What is wrong is smaller and more mechanical: ages, year counts, old names, and a few people standing in two places at once. Six points need your decision. The rest have a one-line fix.

Three things I told you earlier that were wrong

  • Celestine recognising Kael is not a contradiction. I said the script gave three answers. It gives one answer to two different questions. On day 43 "I knew at the furnace" means she knew Kael turned the valve, not Seren. At the end, "One knew you on the steps of the keep" means she recognised the gilded Sorrel as Brennick four years later. Both hold, and both match the bible. Only the master timeline's wording is off (C1, C3).
  • Seren's "I turned the valve. Alone." is not a hole. The game marks it as a lie three times: the narration ("She is very, very careful not to look at you"), Celestine on day 43 ("So it was not her hands on that wheel"), and the Part Two memory where Seren finds the line first and decides somebody who knew should answer for it.
  • Ivo does appear above. He has a line on the barges the night before the Fall ("I held you down. At the furnace."). I had said all his lines were below.

How to lock it in

  1. Decide A2 to A5. Tavi's age, Celestine's age, how the Pairing counts, and Kael's age among the cadets. A1 is already decided (a boy). A6 only needs a yes.
  2. Fix the high ones in the game: B1 Dace, B2 Mara, B3 old names, B4 the year counts. These are the ones a player will notice.
  3. Fix the rest of B and D in one pass. Each is a single line.
  4. Bring the docs into line (C1 to C5), then write your decisions into the story bible, so the book is written from one source.
  5. Then the book. Chapter one already follows the game's lines for the opening; it needs Tavi set to seven, which it has.
