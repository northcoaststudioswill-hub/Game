# Scene 23, The Seat Knife — voice script

For ElevenLabs v3, in Kael's voice. Kael narrates the whole scene; Brand's words are reported ("Brand said"), the same way the Keep memory reports Lucan.

The words are the game's own, with two rule fixes already in: Seren's knife was taken off her and never hung back (C17), and he carried the blade five years, not nine (T6). Captions in the preview film match this script word for word.

Two pauses only, where the scene turns: before Seren's knife, and before the last line.

```
[quiet, low, remembering]
Four years after the furnace. The night before the lift.

[steady, matter-of-fact]
The night before I went up as Edric Sorrel, Brand took the blade the High Auditor had pinned on me with the sun, and put the last hand of it in a forge.

[gruff, slow, Brand's words in Kael's mouth]
Brand said: they gave you a ruler. Straight, so that it can be counted. What did you carry below?

[plain, a little proud]
I said: a seat knife. Every valve hand is issued one. A hooked blade the length of your palm, for cutting a seized seat out of a valve body.

[flat, careful]
It is the only edge soot are allowed. They are numbered. At last bell the knives are counted back onto a board, and a hook left empty on that board is a line struck by morning.

[pause]

[quieter, grief held down]
Seren's was taken off her the night they walked her. I saw it afterwards, in the Warden's tray. Number three hundred and six. Somebody had cleaned it. Nobody hung it back.

[gruff, slow, almost ashamed]
Brand said: I counted that board for eleven years. I never once asked what the knives were for. Hold the tongs.

[low, steady, the work in the voice]
He drew the point out and turned it over the horn until their blade ended in our hook. It took most of the night. He did not speak again until it was in the water.

[gruff, quiet, certain]
Brand said: it is still their steel. It will pass any inspection on that Spire. And every time you draw it, you will know which end is yours.

[pause]

[warm, then hard on the last words]
I called it the bank hook, because nobody up there would know what a bank was. I carried it five years. It is the blade she broke.
```

Expected length: about 75 seconds at a slow read; the film is cut to about 78. Send the audio and the film will be re-cut to it.
