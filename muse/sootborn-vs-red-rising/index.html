SootbornvsRed Rising

The same skeleton with different organs. Sootborn keeps Red Rising's spine (a dead wife, a man remade into a master, a rigged trial, a climb to the top) and then spends its time on the things Red Rising moves past quickly: grief, ordinary life below, and the arithmetic of who gets to breathe.

01 · Timeline overlap

Both clocks start at a death

Each story counted in years from the execution that starts it: the heart-furnace for Seren, the gallows for Eo. Dashed lines join the beats that do the same job.

Sootborn lingersPart One is four days, 41 to 160 days after the furnace. Red Rising has no equivalent stretch: Darrow is cut down and taken to be carved almost at once.

Training takes longerKael has three and a half years between the furnace and the Assay. Darrow reaches the Institute in about one.

The endings line up lateFrom trial to final confrontation is about five years in both. Sootborn's first winter after the Count plays the role Iron Gold plays for Red Rising: the cost of winning.

03 · People

Who stands in for whom

Most of Sootborn's cast splits one Red Rising role into two people or folds two into one. The bar is the same one-to-five closeness score.

In Red Rising, missing from Sootborn

  • A Sevro: the feral best friend at the hero's side through everything.
  • A Mustang romance. Kestrel Dray is the Accredited who asks why, with no love story.
  • A pack like the Howlers. Kael's cadets follow him, but no named band grows up around him.
  • Space. No fleets between planets; the sky is one valley and one tower.

In Sootborn, not in Red Rising

  • Celestine's secret: the ruler was born on Tier Six and won her own trial by shutting a valve.
  • Seren found the bleed first and chose to answer for it, played as her in Bay Three.
  • The Count as a puzzle: Kael rebalances the book and writes his own name in black.
  • A choice at the end: strike her line, or send her home to Row K.
  • Playable side people: Tavi, Mara, Brand, Seren and Celestine each get a level.

07 · Inverse characters

Same place, opposite choice

Sootborn is built in mirrored pairs: two people put in the same position who choose the other way. Red Rising has some of this (Darrow and the Jackal, both climbing Mars), but Sootborn leans on it much harder. These are the inverses inside Sootborn.

08 · Loose threads

The docs disagree here

  • Does Dorian survive the Pairing? The master timeline (4f) says he lives and shares bread on day three. The story bible says the loser leaves "by the other door", puts "Aldane, D." on the wall, and has Maren sell the dock raid "For Dorian Aldane. Balanced." Maren's whole arc depends on which is true.
