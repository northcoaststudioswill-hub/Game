THE IDEA

        
You are the intelligence running a tiny floating island. The citizens look after themselves: they gather, eat, build, sleep, talk and believe things. You nudge.

        
CONTROLS

        
    
          
  • Drag to orbit. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Two fingers (or right-drag) to pan.
    • 
          
  • Pick a power below, then tap the island. Tap the power again to go back to Observe.
    • 
          
  • Powers cost CYCLES, which refill over time and faster when citizens pray.
    • 
        

        
THE SIX POWERS

        
    
          
  • Observe (free): tap a citizen to read their mind. Tap a building to see what it does.
    • 
          
  • Bless: feeds and cheers everyone nearby, regrows trees, berries and crops. Teaches Devotion.
    • 
          
  • Smite: lightning. Kills a citizen, flattens a building, or shatters a rock or tree into instant materials. Everyone watching learns Dread.
    • 
          
  • Create: a new citizen, a grove, rocks, or a blueprint the citizens will build.
    • 
          
  • Inspire: plant one of the four ideas in a citizen. They preach it, and it spreads when people talk.
    • 
          
  • Intervene: drag a citizen through the air. Drop them on a tree, a rock, a building site or a neighbour and they take the hint.
    • 
        

        
THE FOUR IDEAS

        
    
          
  • Devotion (halo): prays, refills your cycles, content.
    • 
          
  • Dread (red hood): works fast and at night, miserable, panics.
    • 
          
  • Acceleration (antenna): studies, makes Insight, forgets to eat.
    • 
          
  • Kinship (sprout): happy, social, more births, will not pray, resists you.
    • 
        

        
When half the island shares one idea, an Age begins and changes the rules for everyone. Opposed ideas argue.

        
THE GOAL

        
Follow the amber objective. It ends at the Spire. What the Spire turns out to be depends on what they believe when it wakes. After that the island is a sandbox.
