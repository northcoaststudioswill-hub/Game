STARHAVEN v0.1.3
Open starhaven-v0.1.3.html in any browser. On a phone, hold it either way up.

Touch: slide a thumb on the left half to move. Buttons on the right: JUMP, STRIKE (or SHOOT), DASH, the down arrow,
FLAME, BOND ART when it appears, TALK when someone has something to say.
Controller: A jump, X strike, B dash, Y talk, left bumper flame, right trigger Bond Art, Start pause.
Keyboard: A/D or arrows move, Space jump, J strike, Shift dash, W talk, F flame, R Bond Art, Esc pause.

The pause menu has "How it works" (every system explained) and Wren's journal.
Saves stay on this device, in this browser.

Old Wyrm: hit its back, not its head. Jump or dash through its charge. When it crouches, leave the shadow on the
ground. Throw fire while it breathes in. A down strike onto its back bounces you clear.
