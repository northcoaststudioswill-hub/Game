# Starhaven v0.1.1 — the playtest build (Nov 18, 2026)

The build for the first playtest by people. Same content as v0.1 (Prologue in two parts, Chapters 1 to 3); this
release is what a played-with-real-taps pass found and fixed, plus the dialogue and menu redesign that followed v0.1.

**Play:** open `starhaven-v0.1.1.html` in a browser, or use the shared Claude link. Phone upright or sideways, touch,
controller or keyboard. **This folder is frozen. Never edit it.**

## Fixed since v0.1

- Consequence cards ("Nessa -10", "This will be remembered") sat on top of explanation cards, the pause menu and the
  journal, including over the "Got it" button. They now hide whenever a menu or card is open.
- The version line on the title read "v0.1 middot; ..." with a second line printed over it.
- The title menu and the pause menu did not fit a sideways phone without scrolling. Both fit now.
- STARFALL did nothing if the Old Wyrm was just off the edge of the screen, and still spent the whole bond bar. The
  boss is always in reach now.
- The Skip button covered dialogue text; it is a tab under the box. Dialogue boxes are as tall as their words.
- Menus are redrawn as writs under a court's seal, coloured by whose realm the chapter is in.
- The goal line and two explanation cards named a screen that had been renamed (it is "Wren's journal").

## For the people playing tonight: what I most want to know

1. **The controls.** Does the left-thumb stick go where you mean? Do you ever strike the wrong way? Is the down
   strike reachable?
2. **The first five minutes.** Three explanation cards arrive early (Starlight, bonds, "this will be remembered").
   Too many, too soon?
3. **The Old Wyrm.** Too long? Did you find the tells (the cliff, the shockwave, fire down its throat)? Did you use a
   Bond Art?
4. **Upright or sideways:** which did you hold it, and was the upright picture too small?
5. **Anything that looked broken**, with the chapter and what was on screen.

## How it was tested

Scripts only: 50 play checks, 19 menu checks, a random-input run (9,000 frames a chapter, both orientations, no
errors), a screenshot run, a speed run (60 pictures a second at full speed; about 33 with the processor slowed
fourfold and no graphics chip), and a run of the opening minutes and the boss with real taps in both orientations.
**It has not been played by a person or run on a phone. Tonight is that test.**

## Known limits

Same as v0.1: the wyrms and wolf are drawn more simply than the cast; four prologue scenes are older drawings; the
upright picture is about a third of the screen; fonts need a connection; sound is placeholder; Chapters 4 to 9 are
not started. Knocked out, you return to the last lantern you lit, so light them.
