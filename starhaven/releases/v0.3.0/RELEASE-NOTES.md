# Starhaven v0.3.0 — The Fallen Sky (Nov 28, 2026)

The Prologue through Chapter 4, in the new world.

**Play:** open `starhaven-v0.3.0.html` in a browser, or use the shared Claude link. **This folder is frozen. Never edit it.**

## What changed

The playtest review said the story mirrored its source novels too closely. Will chose a new world, **The Fallen Sky**
(`WORLD.md`), keeping the plot's shape and changing everything it happens in. Subtitle: *Starhaven: The Fallen Sky*.

- **A new world.** The sky is a country of seven star Houses; its people, the Lit, are born from stars. Mortals live
  Below; the Horizon, a curtain of light that opens at dawn, divides them. Fae, courts, High Lords and the Wall are gone.
- **The hunt:** Wren shoots down a **low star** to sell its light, not a white stag. It was the Dawn House's heir. The
  law is "a light for a light", and Rowan takes her up the Stair through the Horizon (the ride scene, retold).
- **The tyrant:** **Vael, the Eclipse**, who swallows the Houses' light. Her mark is **black glass**, not thorns, and
  her prison is **the Umbra**, not Under the Mountain.
- **The trials:** **the Weighing** (lay one memory on an altar, Father's voice, your first painting or Nessa's laugh,
  and the dark keeps it; no right answer, and the story calls it back later), **the Climb**, and **the Dark** (the
  Swallowing Dark chases you, instead of a wyrm).
- **The rebirth:** Caelum gives dead Wren **half of his own star**, not his blood. Her freckles glow instead of her ears
  turning pointed.
- **The Tether**, not a fated mate bond: Caelum chose to bind her to him without asking. On the rooftop you can be angry,
  ask if it can be undone, or take his hand, and taking his hand **costs** his bond: he wants her to be angry first.
  This is the friction the review said the romance lacked.
- **Other changes:** a **debt of light** instead of "one week of every month"; **falling** like a star instead of
  winnowing; Caelum flies as a **comet** with a starry mantle, not wings; Rowan wears a **crown of morning light**, not
  antlers; **glasslings** instead of thorn sprites; the **starbow**; the letter is sealed with the **Dawn House's
  sunburst**; **the Cartographer** instead of the Weaver.
- **Art:** black glass floors, walls and creatures, a violet-black Umbra, the Swallowing Dark, the low star in the
  level and the scenes, an eclipsed sun, the Eclipse in black glass and violet, Caelum's light leaving his hands, three
  new memory altars, and the new looks for Caelum, Rowan and Wren.
- **Words:** every scene, chapter card, hint, journal page, flight line, film caption and menu motto rewritten.
  `STORY.md` describes the new story; `DESIGN.md` points to `WORLD.md`.

## Limits

- Not played by hand or on a phone. A bot played the whole game, New Game to the end, with no errors, stops or
  knockouts, and its full log was read for leftover old-world words: none remain that a player sees.
- Internal code names (thornSprite, the 'deep' room, wyrm) are unchanged; only what the player sees changed.
- The new writing is yours to read and change: especially the Weighing, the Tether scene and the journal pages.
