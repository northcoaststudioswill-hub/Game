# Starhaven v0.1.10 — menus redrawn, film music, blades, the altar button (Nov 18, 2026)

Same content as v0.1.9.

**Play:** open `starhaven-v0.1.10.html` in a browser, or use the shared Claude link. **This folder is frozen. Never edit it.**

## Changed

- **The menus are redrawn.** They were dark boxes with a gold line round them and rows of small buttons.
  - *Title:* the painted scene (the city, the rooftop, the peak, in turn) now fills the screen at full strength. The
    game's name and a plain list of words stand on its dark side: no box. The chosen word glows, gets a star and an
    underline, and shows its one-line description. Upright, the scene is the top of the screen and the list is below.
  - *Pause:* two columns. Left: where Wren is (chapter, goal, Starlight, choices, level, bonds). Right: what you can
    do, as a list of words with Resume largest. One column upright.
  - *Every other panel* (Constellation, How it works, the chapter card, explanations, the ending): a dark plate with
    gold corners, square, with outlined buttons, in place of the rounded box. The game behind is blurred and dimmed.
- **Music for the opening film.** A 26-second piece written to its shots: thin and cold for the hunt and the Wall,
  low with a slow heartbeat for the Queen and the trials, a rising figure as Caelum's blood wakes the bond, and the
  Starhaven motif as the city appears. The city theme then plays under the title menu, which used to be silent.
- **Wren's blades when she runs.** They were at the ends of swinging arms, so they waved about. Her arms now pump
  close to her body with the elbows bent, and the blades lie back along her forearms in a reverse grip. Same in a dash.
- **Choosing at the Queen's altars on a phone.** The gold button there says CHOOSE but the hint said "the TALK
  button". The hint now names the CHOOSE button, and STRIKE also chooses when you are standing at an altar.
