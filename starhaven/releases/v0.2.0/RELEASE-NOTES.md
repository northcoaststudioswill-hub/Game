# Starhaven v0.2.0 — Chapter 4, The Spy in the Library (Nov 27, 2026)

The Prologue through Chapter 4.

**Play:** open `starhaven-v0.2.0.html` in a browser, or use the shared Claude link. Phone upright or sideways, touch,
controller or keyboard. **This folder is frozen. Never edit it.**

## New

- **Chapter 4, The Spy in the Library, is playable**, end to end, with its own level, enemies, boss, story, evening
  and ending. It follows DESIGN.md's plan for the chapter.
- **The fae bow.** Wren has her bow again, on its own button (BOW on the screen, I on a keyboard, RB on a
  controller). It aims itself at the nearest thing in front of her; hold up to aim high. Bram strung it, as he promised
  in Chapter 2. This is the answer to "there's a bow in the targets but I can't use one".
- **The Library**: a courtyard, then the stacks (wooden galleries three rows apart, each a single jump, bookcases,
  reading tables, globes, arched windows full of night sky), then a domed reading hall. A new indoor look: lamplit
  wood, shelves going up into the dark. The camera looks up a little more here, because things come from above.
- **Winged spies** (new enemy): they hover out of dagger reach, glare red, then dive along a straight line. An arrow
  pins a wing and drops one to the floor, where your daggers finish it. Guarding stops a dive.
- **Lecterns and pages**: in the stacks the spies go for three open books. A spy hovering over a lectern lifts a page
  away (a bar shows how long you have); shoot it off first. Pages you lose are counted on the goal line, change what
  Thane says, and are remembered.
- **The Spymaster's Shadow** (boss): it circles the hall throwing shadow darts (guard them) and swooping low (jump,
  dash, or get on a gallery). Three arrows and its wings fail; it falls, and you cut it on the floor. At half health it
  tears free and comes back faster, needing four arrows. The hint line names each move as it starts.
- **Story**: the bow from Bram, the letter sealed with Spring's rose, a choice about Rowan (remembered), the word
  "Weaver" in the margin, and an evening with Caelum, Thane or Iris. Seven new journal pages. A level 7 perk.
- **The finale and ending screen** now come after Chapter 4 and point to Chapter 5, The Weaver's Cottage. The old
  "something with wings lands on the Library roof" scene now opens Chapter 4.
- Explanation cards now never open mid-fight, near a spy, or during a victory.

## Limits

- Not played by hand or on a phone. A bot played the whole game, New Game to the end, twice; it shoots perfectly, so
  it beat the Shadow in about 16 seconds. A person will take much longer; whether that is fun is the open question.
- The four reading lamps in the stacks are optional and the bot does not light them (Ashes' lamps use the same code).
- The bow aims itself. If that feels like it plays itself, the next step is aiming by hand with the stick.
- Chapter 4's new writing (scripts, evenings, the letter, journal pages) has not been read by you.
