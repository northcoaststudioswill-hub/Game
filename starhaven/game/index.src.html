    Paused

    Keyboard: A/D or arrows move, Space jump, J strike, Shift dash, W talk or paint, S + J in the air strikes down, Esc menu. (Z / X / C also jump / strike / dash.)

    Gamepad: stick or D-pad move, A jump, X strike, B dash, Y talk or paint, Start menu.

    Touch: hold and slide on the left half to move (slide down to aim a downward strike). JUMP, STRIKE and DASH on the right. A TALK or PAINT button appears when there's someone to talk to or something to paint.

    In conversations, tap anywhere or press any button to go on. Skip (or Start / Esc) jumps to the end of the scene or the next choice. For choices, tap one, or use up/down and A (Space on a keyboard).

    Bluetooth controller on a phone: pair it in your phone's Bluetooth settings, open the game, and press any button on it. The touch buttons hide while you use it.
