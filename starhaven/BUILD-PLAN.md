# Starhaven — Build Plan (working with Will)

## How we build this one
- One milestone at a time. Each milestone is played by Will on his phone before the next one starts.
- A milestone is not "done" when the code runs. It is done when Will plays it and says it feels/looks right.
- Art direction is decided BEFORE sprites are drawn, with reference images Will approves. No programmer-art surprises.
- Every build gets: what changed, what was tested, what was NOT tested, and screenshots.

## Quality bar (learned the hard way)
1. Readability first: the player, enemies, ground edges and the next objective must read instantly at phone size.
2. Story before systems: the player always knows who they are, what happened, and what to do next.
3. Feel before content: movement (run / jump / double jump / dash) gets tuned until it feels good before any level is built on top of it.
4. Scope stays sliced: finish the slice, get Will's verdict, then widen.

## Milestones
1. **Movement feel** — Wren in an empty test room: run, jump, double jump, dash. Tuning numbers from the test brief. Nothing else.
2. **Combat feel** — daggers (3-hit combo, air slash, pogo), one thorn sprite, getting hit, hearts.
3. **One mission** — Thornwood: tutorial signs, gaps, 4 lantern checkpoints, motes, end portal, save/load.
4. **Hub stub** — Starhaven street: Rooftop House (rest/save), Caelum with dialogue, mural wall, gate.
5. **Story pass** — opening intro, objective guidance, Caelum bond intro.
6. **First court gift** — Flame, and a thorn wall it burns away.

## Open questions (from the design outline)
- Final names: Wren / Caelum / Starhaven / Court of Dusk — keepers?
- Engine: Godot 4 confirmed? Where does art come from (asset pack, artist, Will's own painting)?
- Prologue tone: genuinely creepy or spooky-cozy?
- Companions: fight alongside Wren, or summonable combo moves only?
- Romance: fixed Caelum path, or player's choice?
