# Starhaven — the story in this concept build

This is what the player sees and does, in order. It follows `DESIGN.md` ("Story outline" and "Chapter goals"):
the first playable is the Prologue through Chapter 2. Every line of dialogue lives in `game/src/story.js`.

## Who's who
- **Wren** (you): a mortal huntress who died in the Deep and woke up fae. Fights with daggers. Used to paint.
- **Nessa and Lissa:** Wren's mortal sisters. Mentioned now, met in Chapter 7.
- **The Thorn Queen:** the fae tyrant who held Wren in the Deep. Gone, but not from Wren's dreams.
- **Lord Caelum:** High Lord of the Court of Dusk, ruler of Starhaven, the romance. Wren owes him a bargain.
- **The Iron King:** across the sea, building an army. His spies and creatures are already in the woods.
- **Bram** and **Iris:** two of the Five, Caelum's inner circle. Bram runs the Training Ring; Iris sees threads of fate.

## Intro (5 painted panels, skippable)
Wren the huntress and her sisters → the Thorn Queen and the Deep, where Wren died → the seven High Lords bring her
back as fae → her bargain with Caelum, who has come to collect → the Iron King's ships, and Starhaven needs everyone.

## Prologue: The Deep (movement only, no enemies)
A nightmare of the Deep. The Thorn Queen taunts Wren as she runs. Teaches move, jump, double jump and dash, then thorns.
Ends with **the Deep Wyrm** waking and chasing her: run right, jump the gaps, reach the light. She wakes up.
- Goal: escape the Deep.

## Chapter 1: Ashes (walk, talk, paint, no enemies)
Wren is living an empty life at another court. Caelum appears and calls in the bargain. He walks her up the road
(winnowing ahead in starlight each time), shows her Starhaven glowing in the distance, and explains the city has gone
dark from the war. At the gate he asks her to paint. She paints the first mural and the whole street lights up.
On the roof of his house: banter, "try not to stab any of them", and Caelum's first bond heart.
- Goals: walk with Caelum to Starhaven; paint the first mural; meet Caelum on his rooftop.

## Chapter 2: The Five (daggers, pogo, first enemies)
Bram tests Wren at the Training Ring: a 3-hit combo on the dummy, then his thorn pit (bounce on the buds with a
downward strike). Iris says hello (optional). Bram sends her through the north gate to clear three thorn sprites,
the Iron King's creatures. Caelum arrives: "Next time it won't be sprites."
- Goals: show Bram your daggers; cross the thorn pit; clear the thorn sprites (count shown).

## Then
An end screen says what comes next if this direction is right: the Starhaven hub, bond evenings, the bow, and Flame.

## How the player always knows what's going on
- The **GOAL line** at the top always shows the chapter and the one thing to do now.
- **Chapter cards** open each chapter (what's happening, the goals) and close it (what changed).
- **Dialogue** with portraits for every story beat. People talk to Wren at the moment it matters.
- **Control hints** appear right where a new move is needed, worded for touch, keyboard or gamepad.
- **Menu > Story & goals:** the goal checklist, the story so far, and everyone you've met.
