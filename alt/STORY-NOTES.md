# Pocket Lantern: story notes from Will

Kept next to the game so the code and the story stay in step. Newest first. These are Will's decisions; anything marked OPEN is still his to decide.

## Nov 17: Guardians of the Light

- The big dimmed buddies are **Guardians of the Light**, not alphas. The name is a north-east Ohio nod.
- Every place has one. They went dim when the Lantern did.
- **A dim Guardian spells peril for the kingdom.**
- When you bring a Guardian the light, its **Sigil stone goes home to the Lantern**.
- In the game since v12.1: every "alpha" the player can read now says "guardian". The code still calls them `alpha` inside.

## Nov 17: why the three of them are at Sunkeep

- **They don't really know why they work at Sunkeep.**
- **Marcus** was on a Flow Pros job with Derek.
- **Will and Erin** were planning their wedding.
- The light went dark and they were **called** to Sunkeep.
- **Will arrived last.** That is why Marcus and Erin fill him in during the tutorial.
- OPEN: Sunkeep has to exist for a reason, and somebody or something called them. To be written later.
- In the game since v12.1: one new page in the opening story, two early lines from Marcus and Erin at Sunkeep, and a few idle lines.

## Nov 17: defeat, the souls, and King Bibi

- A darkened buddy you can't show the light **dies**. No penalty for the player.
- Erin explains why, after the first one, out in the field.
- **King Bibi beams the idea in.** A cut-in in the style of the HASBARA super in Knockout, but Bibi is at the desk and the keeper is the target. The keeper thinks the idea is his own.
- After the cottage is built, the keeper finds the lamp and tells Marcus. Marcus: "You're J-lighting again." J-lighting is Marcus's own word for the keeper taking Bibi's ideas and calling them his.
- HASBARA means explaining. The word stays.
- OPEN: the lamp's real name ("SOUL LAMP" is a placeholder) and what the souls do later.

## Nov 17: expeditions

- Spare sprouts go out from the Lantern to carry the light to far places, like expeditions in Pikmin Bloom. It is also how a crowd of sprouts is kept in check. The base should not be hard, and should not be brain dead either.
