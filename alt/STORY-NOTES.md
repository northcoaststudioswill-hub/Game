## Nov 18: the North Coast and the second light (PROPOSED by Claude, not yet Will's)

Will asked how, lore wise, to introduce the next area in Sprouts. This is what was built. None of it is decided; every
line here is Will's to keep, change or cut. It was written to fit what is already canon and to leave Sproutwild alone.

- **The skip was a call.** The Great Lantern skipping once a night (prequel pass) is the Lantern calling a second
  lantern, and getting no answer.
- **Two lights.** "One to keep. One to call." Sun Keep is the Great Lantern. The second is the NORTH LIGHT, a stone
  tower on a rock in a lake with no far side, at the mouth of the Crooked River. Its carving reads SUN CALL, in the same
  hand as the arch at Willow Falls.
- **The Lanternwisp** at the falls was the North Light's last flame, waiting for a keeper to carry her home. This is
  what "some say she is waiting for someone" was for.
- **Thornback** remembers the two lights when he sees the beam. He still does not remember who set him to guard.
- The lake is the **Wending Lake**, Sproutwild's own name for it (its Bessling is "the lake monster's pup").
- **Bessie** lives in the lake and watches the light. Marcus names her in the scene ("Nobody else name her").
- **A third thing.** Thornback's last line: "Keep. Call. And a third thing, under the mountain. It hummed. I did not
  like it." With a cold light on a far peak. This points at the Data Center under the Peak without naming it.
- OPEN, and the reason for the name: Will's question from Nov 17 was who or what **called** the three of them to
  Sunkeep. A light whose whole job is to call keepers is one possible answer. The game does not say so; it only puts
  the light there.
- OPEN: once relit here, the North Light has to be dark or silent again by Sproutwild, or Sproutwild has to mention it.
- OPEN: the area is east on the map with the lake along its north edge. It is called the NORTH COAST for the lake.

## Nov 17: Guardians of the Light

- The big dimmed buddies are **Guardians of the Light**, not alphas. The name is a north-east Ohio nod.
- Every place has one. They went dim when the Lantern did.
- **A dim Guardian spells peril for the kingdom.**
- When you bring a Guardian the light, its **Sigil stone goes home to the Lantern**.
- In the game since v12.1: every "alpha" the player can read now says "guardian". The code still calls them `alpha` inside.

- The light went dark and they were **called** to Sunkeep.
