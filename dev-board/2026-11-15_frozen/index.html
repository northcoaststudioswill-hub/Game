Waiting on Will

  • Phone verdicts. No version has been confirmed on the iPhone: text placement, touch feel, dash, frame rate.
  • Erin's verdict on Patch & Plate's pace, difficulty and hints.
  • Canon calls: the Sprout King and everything in Patch & Plate are invented, so they need your OK. Ranger and Scout are still placeholder names.
  • Knockout balance and names: Marcus won the fewest CPU matches in the last soak. Mama and Bean are placeholders, and Corbin has a stand-in sprite.
  • "Three little auto balls" you dislike in Rootdeep: still unidentified.

Known soft spots

  • Sprouts' valley projects haven't been tested in a long play-through.
  • The Mothshade caused every death in the bot playtest.
  • Rootdeep rooms clear in 4 to 10 seconds, and difficulty is untouched until you've played more.
  • Rootdeep's music is a placeholder loop and needs a William Cohen track.
  • The Lantern menu still counts three cartridges, not four.
