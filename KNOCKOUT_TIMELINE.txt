KO-1: Artifact v14, 2026-11-14 01:38 UTC, commit e2344a7
"Add North Coast Knockout, a 2D fighter, as cartridge 2"
- Repo set up: the single-file console split into src/shell.html, src/tracks.js and src/sprouts.js, plus build.py to produce index.html.
- Second cartridge slot unlocked: "Knockout", with its own label art.
- One-on-one fighter, best of 3 rounds, 60-second timer, then a finisher.
- 8 fighters: Will, Erin, Louie (standing Shiba), Chrome, Gas Guy, Kiss Man, Points, Mama.
- 8 stages from the user's photos: Living Room, Backyard, Front Lawn, The Trail, The Overpass, Gas Station, Fitting Rooms, Rooftop.
- Moves: punch combo, kick, uppercut, sweep, throw, block, jump attacks, one special per fighter, a super, and a finisher ("-ality").
- Modes: Arcade (7 fights, Louie as final boss) and Quick Fight. Easy, Normal and Hard CPU difficulty.
- Built-in chiptune music.
- Tested with a CPU-vs-CPU soak: 439 rounds, no crashes or stuck matches.

KO-2: Artifact v15, 2026-11-14 04:40 UTC, commit c22a279
"GBA-style sprites, second specials, direction+button controls"
- User feedback: the graphics were too playful and the move sets needed signature moves.
- Look: taller GBA-fighter proportions with smaller heads, shaded limbs, darker outlines, dithered skies, and hit sparks instead of comic words.
- Renames: Chrome became LIL BOTTIE and Kiss Man became CORBIN ("Can I Get A Kiss Man", from Love Island USA season 8).
- A second special for every fighter:
  Will - Mazel Tov Laser
  Erin - Towel Whip (Leash Lasso moved to her second special)
  Louie - Shiba Plane (airplane ears)
  Lil Bottie - Clanker Guns, Chain Stun
  Gas Guy - Tire Pressure
  Corbin - Sneaky Kiss
  Points - Point Blank
  Mama - Mom Voice
- Controls: researched two-button fighters. Moved to direction + button inputs with no motion inputs, and added a double-tap dash.
- Soak test: 427 rounds, no errors.

v16 and v17: Sprouts updates from another session, published by 2026-11-14 11:56 UTC
- One of these is version id 1794657381 (11:56 UTC). The other was not seen from this session.
- Changes were to Sprouts only:
  - Marcus's Waterworks dialogue and dap
  - Thornback becomes a friend (renamed achievement and mission)
  - story scenes, zone pans, travel hop
  - camera and viewport changes
- The Knockout code was unchanged in these versions.

KO-3: Artifact v18, 2026-11-14 15:51 UTC, commits b1e05bd (15:44) and 30fd20e (15:51)
"Four-button layout, heavier hits, Marcus, per-fighter moves, battle theme"
- User feedback: no two-button presses on a phone, fights felt floaty, Erin's head looked off, attacks should be character-specific, add Marcus and the battle song.
- Music: "Retro Battle Theme" (dreamliketextures752) is the only track in Knockout, re-encoded at 128 kbps.
- Controls: X and Y face buttons appear only while Knockout is inserted, so Sprouts keeps its two-button layout.
  A = light (A,A,A combo), B = heavy, X = special 1, Y = special 2, Select = super (it glows when ready).
  Finisher: X, Y or Select.
- Impact: higher gravity and a faster fall, longer hitstop, a white hit flash, victim shake, stronger screen shake, a zoom punch on big hits, more knockback, and landing dust.
- Erin: head redrawn, and her down + A is the "Bean Dip".
- Louie: Paw Paw Paw, Chomp (bite), Need More Loaf (loaf slide), Zoom Zoom Zoom (X), Turbo Zoom (Y), and a new super, Judgement (giant Shiba side-eye).
- MARCUS replaces Points. His moves:
  - normals: Bro Jab, Pipe Wrench, Plunger Pop, Snake the Drain
  - Dab Cloud (X)
  - Double or Nothing (Y, a coin flip)
  - Hydro Jet super
  - Flush-ality finisher
- Every fighter has named basic attacks. Corbin's are all "Can I Get A Kiss".
- A move list screen opens from the pause menu, or with X or Y on character select.
- Merge: the v16/v17 Sprouts changes were kept, and the Knockout hooks were reapplied on top (commit 30fd20e).
- Soak test: 449 rounds, no errors. Sprouts was checked to still boot into its new opening scene.
