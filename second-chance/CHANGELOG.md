# Second Chance: changelog

## v0.1.0 (Nov 19 2026): first vertical slice, third person

Built clean from the camera and gameplay brief. Third-person 3D action-adventure; there is no first-person view.

**Camera**
- Follows from behind and above with the whole body in frame; eased touch-drag rotation; drifts back behind the
  runner when left alone.
- Shortens near walls and climbs instead of crowding when there is no room behind.
- Pulls back in combat; optional lock-on with a marker on the target.
- Staged angles only for cutscenes, finishers and story beats.

**Look**
- Cel shading in three flat tones, the middle one printed as halftone dots; hard shadows; ink outlines on the
  characters, props and buildings; drawn brick, window, crate and container textures.
- Dez Calloway: a skinned low-poly model with a trench coat, a scarf that streams when he runs, a white streak in
  his hair, a marked left arm that takes the path colour, and a drawn face with seven expressions.
- Comic lettering on impacts (WHAM!, FWOOM!, SHHK!, SNAP!, KA-THOOM!), burst shapes, ink splats, speed lines.
- Dialogue in comic panels with speaker pictures rendered from the models; the opening is a three-panel page.

**Play**
- Run, three-hit melee combo with lunge and soft targeting, dodge roll with a late-dodge slowdown, interact.
- Three paths, each with a power the character visibly performs: Ember (Pyre), Shade (Shadowstep), Tether (Grave
  Chain), each with a passive and a second rank from clues.
- Finishers on staggered Bruisers and the boss, with their own camera.
- The second chance: one revival, restored at crossings.
- Enemies: Hollow, Knifer, Bruiser, Deacon Voss (two phases).
- One connected map: alley, street, freight yard, warehouse interior with a mezzanine, three rooftops joined by a
  plank and a scaffold bridge. Five clues, three doors, three crossings, breakable crates.

**Controls**
- Phone: left-thumb stick, right-thumb camera, Attack, Dodge, Power, Interact and Lock buttons on the right;
  landscape first, portrait works.
- Keyboard and mouse, and gamepad.

**Tested and not tested:** see the README. Scripted full playthroughs pass on all three paths. No person has
played it yet and it has not run on a real phone.
