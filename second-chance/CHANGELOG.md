# Second Chance: changelog

## v0.3.0 (Nov 28 2026): the case turns personal

A mystery-thriller pass: a hero who jokes when it is worst, a friend on the radio, a witness to talk round, and a
case that turns out to be about family. All characters are new to this game.

**Story**
- Josie: Dez's sister, gone eleven years. Her photograph, dated last Thursday, is in the satchel Voss stole. Found
  in the warehouse.
- A last page after the boss when the photo has been found: Josie and Mother Mercy, the same night.
- Four chapter cards: Eleven Forty-Seven, Pier 9, The Ledger, Everybody Pays.

**Characters**
- Nell Szabo, night dispatcher, on the radio at six points in the night. Radio calls play while you keep moving.
- Otis Greer, bartender at the Lethe, in his side door after the alley fight. A conversation with three replies
  (joke, lean on him, pay him), each with its own answer and bonus.
- Dez gets one-liners in a speech balloon: finishers, second chances, climbing, Ruth.

**Investigation**
- The case board: clue cards with pictures of where each was found. Pin two together; four deductions and one
  extra, 10 scrip each. Opens from a CASE button or the pause menu.
- Seven clues now (Otis's story and Josie's photo added); still three for the second rank of your power.

**Fixes**
- Handrails on all three bridges: a hard knock in the boss fight could throw Dez off the scaffold bridge.
- Otis's space runs to the wall, so Dez cannot be wedged behind him.
- The radio and speech balloons hide under menus and story pages.

## v0.2.0 (Nov 28 2026): echoes, rooftops, the ledger

Built out from the v0.1 slice. Same block of the city, same camera rule (third person, always), more to do in it.

**Investigation**
- Echoes: two clues now replay the past with see-through ink figures on the real set, lights down, watched from
  behind Dez. The alley shows his murder (a man in a white coat and wide hat, three shots, counted out loud). The
  warehouse office shows who Voss answers to: Mother Mercy, seen for the first time.

**Exploration**
- Climbable ladders: both tenements over Halloran Alley and the container stack in the Pier 9 yard. Dez climbs
  hand over hand and mantles over the top; Interact at the top climbs back down.
- The two tenement roofs, joined by a plank across the alley: laundry lines, a stair hut, water towers, antennas.
- Ruth Okafor, a ghost on the west roof, and a side quest for her locket. Returning it lets her go, with a reward.
- Eight hidden IOUs from Mr. Tally around the block.

**Combat**
- Counter: Attack inside the late-dodge slowdown; Dez crosses the gap and lands a heavy blow.
- Past Due, a fourth combo hit (bought): a leap and a slam with a ring of force.
- The Caller: a new enemy who keeps its distance and lobs burning lamp oil at where you are heading. A red ring
  shows where it lands.
- Lamp-oil drums on the street, in the yard, in the warehouse and on the roofs. They chain into each other.
- Wall splats when a Hollow is knocked hard into something solid.

**Progression and settings**
- Scrip, dropped by Hollows and crates, spent in Tally's ledger at crossings on five upgrades.
- Challenge setting (Story, Normal, Hard) in the pause menu. Sound, camera speed, picture and challenge are kept on
  the device.
- The pause menu shows IOUs found and the side quest; the end card shows counters, IOUs, Ruth and the challenge.

**Fixes**
- Ladders and other things to use get their own marker, so the red "!" only ever means an attack is coming.
- Markers hide during staged shots.

**Tested and not tested:** see the README. Six full scripted runs pass (three paths, Hard, Story, no dodging).
Still not played by a person or run on a real phone.

## v0.1.0 (Nov 19 2026): first vertical slice, third person

Built clean from the camera and gameplay brief. Third-person 3D action-adventure; there is no first-person view.

**Camera**
- Follows from behind and above with the whole body in frame; eased touch-drag rotation; drifts back behind the
  runner when left alone.
- Shortens near walls and climbs instead of crowding when there is no room behind.
- Pulls back in combat; optional lock-on with a marker on the target.
- Staged angles only for cutscenes, finishers and story beats.

**Look**
- Cel shading in three flat tones, the middle one printed as halftone dots; hard shadows; ink outlines on the
  characters, props and buildings; drawn brick, window, crate and container textures.
- Dez Calloway: a skinned low-poly model with a trench coat, a scarf that streams when he runs, a white streak in
  his hair, a marked left arm that takes the path colour, and a drawn face with seven expressions.
- Comic lettering on impacts (WHAM!, FWOOM!, SHHK!, SNAP!, KA-THOOM!), burst shapes, ink splats, speed lines.
- Dialogue in comic panels with speaker pictures rendered from the models; the opening is a three-panel page.

**Play**
- Run, three-hit melee combo with lunge and soft targeting, dodge roll with a late-dodge slowdown, interact.
- Three paths, each with a power the character visibly performs: Ember (Pyre), Shade (Shadowstep), Tether (Grave
  Chain), each with a passive and a second rank from clues.
- Finishers on staggered Bruisers and the boss, with their own camera.
- The second chance: one revival, restored at crossings.
- Enemies: Hollow, Knifer, Bruiser, Deacon Voss (two phases).
- One connected map: alley, street, freight yard, warehouse interior with a mezzanine, three rooftops joined by a
  plank and a scaffold bridge. Five clues, three doors, three crossings, breakable crates.

**Controls**
- Phone: left-thumb stick, right-thumb camera, Attack, Dodge, Power, Interact and Lock buttons on the right;
  landscape first, portrait works.
- Keyboard and mouse, and gamepad.

**Tested and not tested:** see the README. Scripted full playthroughs pass on all three paths. No person has
played it yet and it has not run on a real phone.
