## How Rootdeep is wired into the console (slot 3, cart index 2)

Eight small hooks in the HTML, outside `src/rootdeep.js`:
shelf slot 3 + `cartArt3`, `cartPick` allows index 2, boot → `g3StartTitle()`,
`update()`/`draw()` hand any `g3*` state to Rootdeep, `musWant()` stays silent in `g3*`
(Rootdeep plays its own synth tune), `PAD[2]` button labels, `setPad` shows the X/Y buttons
for slot 2 and 3, and `window.__g.RD()/.RDF()` test handles.
Only the Rootdeep block came from the uploaded Sprouts+Rootdeep v2.9 file; its older Sprouts was dropped.

## What changed in Rootdeep this pass

**Keepers now use their Knockout moves, on Knockout's buttons.** A attack, B cast, X special 1,
Y special 2, super = B when the meter is full (fills from your hits, a little when hit).
A freeze-frame cut-in banner plays before each super. Combo finishers call out the keeper's
Knockout heavy (SNEAKER KICK, GARDEN KICK, CHOMP, PIPE WRENCH).

| Keeper | X | Y | Super |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Will | Selfie Flash (stun ring) | Mazel Tov Laser (piercing beam) | Bike Lane (rides through the room) |
| Erin | Towel Whip (roots, shoves) | Leash Lasso (yanks nearest foe in) | Sprout Stampede (6 sprouts charge) |
| Louie | Zoom Zoom Zoom (3 auto-dashes) | Turbo Zoom (long charge) | Judgement (light pillars on every foe) |
| Marcus | Dab Cloud (drifting daze cloud) | Double or Nothing (coin-flip punch) | Hydro Jet (6 geysers in a line) |

Names are canon (straight from Knockout's roster). How each plays top-down is my adaptation, so flag anything that feels wrong.
**The Boof is gone.** It was lil Bottie's placeholder; Louie's real moves are the Zooms.
With no cast boon, B fires a plain lantern bolt.

**Bug fixed:** piercing shots (Will's old laser, Marcus's jet, the Dew god's wave) hit the same foe
every frame they overlapped it, so one shot landed ~5 times. Every shot now keeps a hit list.
That makes the Dew wave weaker than before; watch whether Dew casts still feel good.

**Text fixes:** pause screen no longer prints the currency line over the menu; boon cards size to
the screen and never spill; character select fits and shows each keeper's X / Y / super;
the bottom help text on Keepsakes, Ledger, Mirror and Armory no longer overlaps descriptions.
