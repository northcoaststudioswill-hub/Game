## How Rootdeep is wired into the console (slot 3, cart index 2)

Eight small hooks in the HTML, outside `src/rootdeep.js`:
shelf slot 3 + `cartArt3`, `cartPick` allows index 2, boot → `g3StartTitle()`,
`update()`/`draw()` hand any `g3*` state to Rootdeep, `musWant()` stays silent in `g3*`
(Rootdeep plays its own synth tune), `PAD[2]` button labels, `setPad` shows the X/Y buttons
for slot 2 and 3, and `window.__g.RD()/.RDF()` test handles.
Only the Rootdeep block came from the uploaded Sprouts+Rootdeep v2.9 file; its older Sprouts was dropped.

## Playtest pass (from Will's first play on his iPhone)

Will loved the intro (the ground splitting under the Lantern). His notes and what changed:
- **"Everyone starts at the bottom, cluttered."** Every Hearth position was an offset from the bottom, so on a tall phone everything piled into the bottom third.
  The Hearth now lays out in fractions of the screen height (`hubL()`, `NPCPOS`/`NPCROUTE` y values are fractions): stations on the top wall,
  Erin's sprout bed upper left, Marcus's corner upper right, the rug and Louie in the middle, Scout's desk lower left, Ranger's camp lower right,
  a clear lane to the root door. You start in the middle.
- **"Something interactive on the levels."** Chambers now hold Sprouts-style things (`rdInts`): pots to smash (clay, crystal, ember urns by area),
  buried sprouts to pluck with A, berry bushes to eat with A, root chests to open with A (elite chambers always have one), and boom bulbs that
  fizz when hit and blow up nearby foes (and chain). A only uses a thing when no foe is within reach; otherwise it attacks.
- **"Sprouts should swarm."** Buds now leave the string, spread across foes near you, latch on and chew in small bites, hop to the next foe, and
  come home when the room is clear. PEB stays as your guard. Foes that blink, charge or leap shake them off. Bites stay small on purpose
  (`RDBITE`), and don't spam damage numbers. Royal Whistle calls them home for a moment.
- **Marcus's voice, locked in** (see below). His Rootdeep lines were rewritten; Sprouts' lines are untouched (they're the canon).
- **The roguelite loop Will didn't reach** is tested end to end: death banks Moondew, keys and honey; the Mirror upgrade carries into the next
  run; ten boons kept across chambers; both bosses pay keys; chamber 12 escapes; the Pact opens; heat carries; everything saves.

### Marcus, voice guide (both games)
Sprouts' Marcus is the canon: a cocky "bro" hype man with big ideas and a bigger ego (Marcus Waterworks, "I've got a guy. The guy is me.",
"See one, send one", "Dap me up"). In Rootdeep he keeps all of that and roasts everyone and everything down there. Every line should have both:
the bro swagger and the roast. Say "bro" a lot. He brags about himself even while trash-talking. He is loyal under it (he'd never say so).
**Continuity rule:** Marcus never mentions Waterworks (or its lazy river) until it exists in Sprouts. In Rootdeep any such line is a
`[before, after]` pair resolved by `rdLn()`; `rdWaterworks()` is true once Sprouts' MAKING WAVES achievement is earned (saved for good)
or the Waterworks project is finished in the current valley. Apply the same rule to any future line that leans on Sprouts progress.

## Text-in-box pass

- **Root cause:** text used a 'top' baseline, which places glyphs by the font's em box. iPhone's SF Mono puts the letters a few pixels lower
  than desktop fonts, so on the phone text sat on the bottom edge of tags and boxes (and looked fine in headless tests).
  The shared `text()` in the console HTML now places Rootdeep's text by measured cap height (`capOf`), so letter tops land exactly at y on any font.
  It only does this while cartridge 3 is in; Sprouts' text is untouched.
- Hearth talk box grows to fit up to four lines (long lines used to be cut off) with its footer inside; the nameplate uses the speaker's colour.
- HUD health is a taller bar with the number centred in it. List rows sit centred in their selection boxes. Character-select names and tags share
  a centre line. Boon cards size to their text. The title's run count no longer shows through the menu box. Mirror descriptions read as sentences.
