## How Rootdeep is wired into the console (slot 3, cart index 2)

Eight small hooks in the HTML, outside `src/rootdeep.js`:
shelf slot 3 + `cartArt3`, `cartPick` allows index 2, boot → `g3StartTitle()`,
`update()`/`draw()` hand any `g3*` state to Rootdeep, `musWant()` stays silent in `g3*`
(Rootdeep plays its own synth tune), `PAD[2]` button labels, `setPad` shows the X/Y buttons
for slot 2 and 3, and `window.__g.RD()/.RDF()` test handles.
Only the Rootdeep block came from the uploaded Sprouts+Rootdeep v2.9 file; its older Sprouts was dropped.

Y special 2, super = B when the meter is full (fills from your hits, a little when hit).
A freeze-frame cut-in banner plays before each super. Combo finishers call out the keeper's
Knockout heavy (SNEAKER KICK, GARDEN KICK, CHOMP, PIPE WRENCH).

the screen and never spill; character select fits and shows each keeper's X / Y / super;

## Graphics overhaul pass (same day, later)

Art direction and the fix list live in `handoffs/ROOTDEEP_ART_MANIFESTO.md`. Done this pass:
- Every sprite at 1x (bosses at an exact 2x, Hollow Keeper = Will's sprite at 2x in shadow). Elites get a gold outline.
- 16px biome floors and a real top wall per biome (Burrows earth, Vault wet stone with puddles, Core basalt with ember cracks).
- Grid-snapped props (boulders, stumps, glowing mushrooms, crystals, ember rocks) with matching collision.
- Lantern lighting: a dark layer with stepped light pools. Wall lanterns relight one by one, with a chime, when a chamber is cleared, and the room brightens.
- Reward doors are burrow arches in the wall. The Hearth uses Sprouts' wood floor and warm light.
- Foes and bosses redrawn (`RDFOE` in `src/rootdeep.js`). Grub Mother and Drowned King hitboxes grew to match their 2x size (r 20 and 18).
- **New keeper: the SPROUT KING** (id `KING`). Buds x2 damage, a string of 8, starts each run with 2 buds.
  X Sprout Toss (a bud flies at a foe, latches on and chews), Y Royal Whistle (call buds home, heal, 4s frenzy),
  super Bloom of Kings (buds at 2x size and 3x strength for 6s, plus 3 temporary golden buds that never bank).
  Unlocks after carrying 6 buds in one run (`RD.stats.fullString`). He is new canon, invented this session.

Still placeholder: title screen art, Rootdeep's dialogue box (no portraits yet), the Hearth's sprout bed and rug (flat boxes).

## UI, cinematics and capsules pass

- **Text audit at iPhone 16 Pro Max size** (440x956 CSS, which the console sizes to a 240x304 logical screen).
  The console HTML has a test hook: `window.__g.tb.on(true)` records every text box drawn each frame
  (`__g.tb.last()`), and opening the page with `?dev` adds `__g.ev(code)` to run code inside the game.
  All 170 screens checked (every Rootdeep screen, menu row, boon, shop, cinematic panel, and Sprouts' capsules and settings): no overlaps, nothing squashed or off-screen.
  The ROOTDEEP logo's drop shadow is the only intended overlap.
- **Cinematics** (`RDCINES` in `src/rootdeep.js`), each plays once and is saved in `RD.flags`: each boss the first time you meet it
  (the chamber darkens, the boss rises, its name lands, Marcus or Scout on the radio), the first escape (out into the daylit valley,
  then Marcus pointing you at the Pact), and the Sprout King's arrival at the Hearth after you earn him. START skips.
- **Capsules:** Rootdeep has 11 items in the CAPSULES machine on Sprouts' main menu, in the same no-duplicate pool and odds as the styles:
  four lantern colours (set in Rootdeep's Settings under LANTERN COLOUR), a Moondew Jar, invites that unlock Erin, Louie, Marcus and the Sprout King, and the Root Maul and Thornbow.
  They're saved in Sprouts' save as `meta.own.rd`, so they sync with the rest.
- **Reset pulls:** Sprouts' Settings has RESET PULLS next to BACK. Press A twice. Everything from capsules goes back in the machine and every pull comes back as a ticket.
  Starting styles and BUILT (valley project) items stay. The Moondew Jar's dew isn't taken back.
