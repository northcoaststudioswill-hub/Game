# Pocket Lantern v0.2

A pocket handheld console for your phone or computer, with three cartridges. Open `pocket-lantern.html` in Safari (or any browser) and it runs.

## Opening it

- **iPhone:** tap the file in Files and choose Open in Safari. To put it on your home screen: Share, then Add to Home Screen.
- **Computer:** double-click the file.
- Everything runs inside the file. No internet needed.

## The cartridges

Pick one off the shelf. SWAP CARTRIDGE on each game's menu takes you back to the shelf.

Under the cartridges is the **Lantern menu** (press down, or tap it): one capsule machine for every game, stats from all three games, and Lantern achievements that span the games and pay capsule tickets. Every game earns tickets now.

- **Sprouts:** the cozy one. Gather sprouts, bring home sun shards, build up your house and the valley.
- **North Coast Knockout:** a 2D fighter with four face buttons.
- **Rootdeep (new):** a roguelite under the Sprouts valley. Dive down through 12 chambers, fall, come back up to the Hearth stronger, go again.

## Rootdeep in one minute

- Start a **New Game**. Erin will tell you what's going on. The Root Door at the bottom of the Hearth takes you down.
- **A** attacks (it aims for you), **B** casts, **X** and **Y** are your keeper's special moves, **double-tap a direction** to dash.
  When the SUPER bar is full, **B** fires your super.
- Sprouts you find down there fight for you. Walk over them to pick them up; press **A** next to a buried one to pluck it.
- Pots, berry bushes, chests and boom bulbs: smash them, eat them, open them, or set them off near enemies.
- When you fall, the Moondew you found comes home with you. Spend it in the **Burrow Beneath** (the hatch by the Hearth's back wall), where your sprouts dig you permanent upgrades. Your sprouts come home too.
- When your sprout string is full, extra sprouts you find head up to the surface between chambers and wait for you in the Ledger.
- **Start** pauses. **Save & Quit** keeps your run; **Continue** on the title menu picks it back up.
- Unlock more keepers (Erin, Louie, Marcus, the Sprout King) by playing, or from **Capsules** (the same machine from any menu).

## Controls on a computer

Arrow keys move. Z = A, X = B, C = X, V = Y, Enter = Start, Shift or Q = Select.

## Good to know

- Saves live in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data erases them. Keep the file, not the save.
- The Sprouts title music streams from the original page, so a downloaded copy plays without it. Everything else, including Knockout's and Rootdeep's music, is built in.
- This is a pre-alpha build (v0.2). Things will change.
