# Rico: Findings with Code Causes

I read `src/*.js` and `README.md` after quitting the driver. Each line below gives the blind finding (same numbering as `rico-findings-blind.md`), its severity, and the cause in the code. **WRONG** means the blind finding was mistaken. **PARTLY WRONG** means the symptom is real but my explanation was wrong. A design choice is labelled as such.

## Bugs
1. **major: Reward door blocked by a pillar. CONFIRMED.** The cause is in `rdArena`, game.js:129-131. Pillars are placed with `y` anywhere from 38 and `x` from 22 to W-22. The only areas kept clear are the room centre and the bottom strip. The doors sit at y=26 and x=W/2±38 (game.js:169), and the door trigger is centred at y=34 with radius 13 (game.js:602). The player is clamped to y≥32 and has a radius of 7 (game.js:237-239). So any pillar whose top edge is at y≈38 under a door leaves a gap of about 6 units, which the player can't fit through. Nothing in the code keeps the door approach clear.
2. **major: Attack input skips the death screen. CONFIRMED.** The death screen (`over` state) accepts A or Start after only 0.8 s (game.js:654). Death itself is instant: `damagePlayer` calls `rdDie` in the same frame (game.js:401-407, 196), with no slow-motion or input buffer. Players who are mashing attack dismiss the summary.
3. **minor: Mission completes on the first frame of a run. CONFIRMED, cause is design.** Missions count lifetime stats: `stn('elites')` for TAKE THE ELITE DOORS (story.js:80) and `honeyFound` for SNACKS (story.js:67). `missionWatch` runs once per second in a run (game.js:518) and announces "MISSION DONE" the moment an already-satisfied mission is checked (story.js:168). Progress made before you accepted the mission counts, so the mission is done on frame 1.
4. **minor: Boon text doesn't match its effect. CONFIRMED.** Passive boon descriptions are static strings: Kindled Heart is "+25 max health" (data.js:34). But the value applied is `v*rar*level` (game.js:79-80), and `boonDesc` returns the static `p.d` for passives (game.js:87), so a Rare Kindled Heart gives +36 while the card says +25. The BUILD screen prints `dmgMul` (ui.js:195), which only includes the Mirror's Might. Attack boons feed `atkMul` (game.js:72-73), so "Attack +51%" next to "DMG x1.00" is a display mismatch.
5. **minor: Dash boon text reads like debug output. CONFIRMED (text bug).** In `boonDesc`, game.js:86, `(b.slot==='atk'?'Attack +':'Cast +')` sends the dash slot into the "Cast +v%" branch, then appends "Dash power v". Dash boons don't change cast at all (`castMul` only reads `b.cast`, game.js:73). So "Cast +14%" on a dash boon is false, and "Dash power 14" is a raw number.
6. **minor: The super button opens the echo card. PARTLY WRONG.** The echo isn't opened by Y. It opens by **proximity**: any keeper within 12 units (game.js:519). The echo spawns at the room centre ±30 (story.js:193), right where you are standing when the last foe dies. The state switches to `echo`, so the super press in that frame is lost. The symptom is real; the trigger is walking or standing on it, not the button.
7. **minor: Door label doesn't match the boon giver. CONFIRMED, but my description was slightly off.** The door's label uses `d.god` (ui.js:102). The boon screen header uses `boonOffer[0].god` (ui.js:180). When a duo is offered, it is put first and `mkBoon` sets its god to `d.gods[0]` (game.js:97, 103). Wildfire Storm is EMBER+ZIP, so a PEB door shows "EMBER SPEAKS".
8. **minor: Simultaneous super only fires for one player. CONFIRMED.** `doSuper` returns silently if `RUN.freeze>0` (moves.js:49). The first super sets a 0.6 s freeze and a single `freezeP` slot (moves.js:51; game.js:521), so a second player's press in that window is dropped. Their meter isn't spent, but they get no feedback.
9. **minor: Super sometimes needs a second press. COULD NOT CONFIRM.** One candidate is `movesInput` returning early during a dash-strike dive or a Louie zoom, before it checks Y (moves.js:88-98). Another is the freeze check (moves.js:49). I can't tie either to shot 804/805 for certain.
10. **minor: The first A press on some modals is eaten. CONFIRMED, cause is a deliberate lockout.** The boon screen ignores A for 0.35 s (game.js:309). Echo cards ignore input for 0.5 s (story.js:196), and the "A: KEEP IT" prompt only blinks in after 0.5 s (ui.js:175). These lockouts are reasonable, but there's no visual cue that input is locked.
11. **nit: Death screen shows the wrong biome. CONFIRMED.** Behind the death and win screens the game draws `sceneTitle`, not the room you died in (models.js:414), because `RUN` is already null.
12. **nit: Run starts with a projectile already in flight. CONFIRMED.** Enemies spawn with a 0.5 s `born` timer, but `e.cd-=dt` runs before the born check (game.js:462). Spitters start with `cd` of 1-2 s (game.js:137) and have a 125-unit range (game.js:470), so they can fire around 0.5 s into the room while the biome logo (`zoneT` 2 s, ui.js:122) is still covering the screen.
13. **nit: Inconsistent heal after the Grub Mother. RESOLVED: keepsake effect, not a bug.** A full heal after a boss only happens with the PRESSED FLOWER keepsake (game.js:175; data.js:67). I had it equipped in run 2; the save reset removed it. Small issue: no on-screen text says the keepsake healed you.

## Combat feel / fairness
14. **major: The player character vanishes. CONFIRMED.** While `p.inv>0`, the keeper model is hidden on alternate 1/14 s frames (models.js:265). Hits give 0.55 s of `inv` (game.js:398). A super gives 1.4 s (moves.js:50). Death Defiance gives 2 s and a revive 1.5 s (game.js:404, 527). For those whole windows the player strobes, and about half of all frames show only the ring. Foes and the Boulderfall burst draw over the player with no outline or silhouette pass (models.js:341).
15. **major: Enemy projectiles look like pickups. CONFIRMED (PARTLY WRONG about the Vault).** Every enemy shot in every biome is the same salmon sphere with a pink glow sprite (`#ff7a6b`, models.js:371). On the pink Burrows floor that reads the same as petal and heart pickups (models.js:338-339). The Vault shots aren't blue; they are the same salmon orbs. What I saw as blue was crabs and Mist effects.
16. **major: Body-contact damage is large and not telegraphed. CONFIRMED.** `touchHit` (game.js:460-461) deals full `e.dmg` on overlap with no wind-up, and every foe and boss calls it every frame (game.js:481, 513). Boss damage scales with chamber depth: `dmg*sc.dmg` with `sc.dmg=1+.09*(ch-1)` (game.js:134, 140). That makes the Grub Mother ≈16.5 and the Drowned King ≈24.5 per touch, and both chase you (game.js:492, 498). The King's leap has a 0.7 s telegraph and ring (game.js:500), but body contact has none.
17. **major: No warning before a boss, and no heal before it. CONFIRMED.** Bosses are fixed at chambers 4, 8 and 12 (game.js:148). The doors before them come out of `chamberCleared`, which forces one door to `boon` at chambers 3, 7 and 11 (game.js:168). The other door is random from 9 kinds, so a Fountain shows up only about 1 in 9 times (game.js:166-167). The door labels never say "BOSS" (ui.js:102).
18. **minor: Sprout-spitters kite at the player's walk speed. PARTLY WRONG.** Spitters back off when closer than 70 units at their own speed of 25 (game.js:468). The player moves at 92 (game.js:535), so they can be caught. My whiffs were mostly aim or facing (see 19). Dash strike does fix it. That it's only taught in the pause menu is finding 28.
19. **minor: Melee reach and facing are unclear. CONFIRMED.** The Petal Blade's range is 27 plus the foe's radius, in a ±57° arc (data.js:54; game.js:412-413). Aim is the last non-zero stick or key direction (game.js:361-362), with no melee aim assist. On keyboard that means swinging at whatever direction you last walked.
20. **minor: Cast aim-assist overrides the stick. CONFIRMED.** Boulderfall drops on the nearest foe within 140 regardless of aim (game.js:442). Sky Judgement hits the nearest 3 (game.js:440).
21. **minor: Bolt Step auto-redirects the dash. WRONG.** ZIP's dash boon only zaps the nearest foe within 95 (game.js:424-425); dash movement still follows your aim (game.js:418-420). I misread the zap line as movement.
22. **minor: Thornbow is underpowered. CONFIRMED.** Arrows do 8 damage, the bow fires every 0.21 s, and arrows don't pierce (data.js:56; game.js:411). Hold-A heavy on the bow is the same 32-range melee kick as every weapon (moves.js:70-74), so a bow heavy at range does nothing.
23. **minor: Root Maul swings lock you in place. WRONG.** Nothing in the code locks movement or dash during a swing; `p.swing` is visual only (game.js:410, 535, 540). Holding attack does slow you to 55% while charging (game.js:535), which may be what I felt.
24. **minor: The shield bubble gives no feedback. CONFIRMED.** A shielded crab takes ×0.35 from everything except burn, heavy and super (game.js:366). There is no "SHIELDED" text, just small numbers. README:88 says heavies break shields, but the game never tells you.
25. **minor: Damage numbers overlap. CONFIRMED.** Numbers get only ±4 units of x jitter (game.js:371) and stack with banners (`say2`, ui.js:123) and popups (game.js:415).
26. **nit: Bud scatter/rejoin is unexplained. CONFIRMED, and worse than I thought.** Every hit has a 30% chance to **permanently** remove a bud from the string (game.js:399). They don't come back; the "JOINED" I saw was a new pod. The HUD line hides when you have none (ui.js:116). Peb's block has a 15 s cooldown (game.js:395-396).
27. **nit: Co-op isn't easier. CONFIRMED, cause is design.** Each extra keeper adds `(2+ch*.5)` to the chamber budget and +75% boss HP (game.js:150, 139). Death Defiance only fires to stop a full wipe (game.js:403-404; README:40-46).

## Controls / UI
28. **major: Dash strike is only taught in the pause menu. CONFIRMED.** The CH1 hint (ui.js:118-119) lists hit, cast, dash, heavy, special and super, but not dash strike. Dash strike needs attack within the dash or 0.16 s after it (moves.js:87, 100). README:18 documents it; in-game it's only in the pause controls page.
29. **minor: Hub hint is missing the keyboard key. WRONG.** ui.js:73 prints `( , ON KEYS)` on purpose: `,` (comma) is the Keyboard-2 join key (input.js:81). It's a real key, but rendered alone in parentheses it looks like a missing glyph. Clarity nit only.
30. **minor: Keyboard is ignored while a pad is player 1. CONFIRMED, cause is design.** Only joined devices drive players (input.js:74-82). Pressing Z or Enter on Keyboard-1 joins as a new player (input.js:82), which is how I got "PLAYER 2 JOINED".
31. **minor: Keeper Select text overlaps itself. CONFIRMED.** The per-keeper block under the model (tag, signature, stats, then "A: READY") flows downward from the model's foot (ui.js:231-233). The solo description is pinned at VH-40/VH-28 (ui.js:234-235) and the footer at VH-13 (ui.js:236). At 960x540 they collide.
32. **minor: The CH1 control hint covers the fight. PARTLY WRONG.** It only shows during chamber 1 for the first 7 s of game time (ui.js:118). It does sit at VH*0.2, over the arena. With the driver's frame stepping I saw it in most CH1 shots, but it doesn't stay forever.
33. **minor: Station prompts need precise standing. CONFIRMED (PARTLY WRONG about body-blocking).** Interaction radius is 17 units, and stations sit at y=38 against the back wall (game.js:240-241). NPCs compete in the same nearest-target pick (game.js:242), so a wandering NPC or Louie, who follows the player on route 'P3' (game.js:224, 254), steals the A prompt from DESCEND or a station. There's no player-NPC collision, so "body-blocking" was really prompt-stealing.
34. **minor: Sprout pods aren't explained. WRONG (mechanism).** Pods are collected by walking within 12 units (game.js:561), not by hitting them. My attack lunges and the special's movement just carried me into range. The real issue is the small pickup radius with no prompt.
35. **minor: Banners linger over the arena. CONFIRMED.** "AN ELITE GUARDS THIS CHAMBER" shows for 2 s (game.js:157) over the 2 s biome logo (ui.js:122). The boss subtitle box sits at VH-50 (ui.js:126).
36. **minor: Honey's purpose is unclear until the shop. CONFIRMED, cause is design.** Honey is a gift currency: Start mid-talk gifts it (game.js:280-282; README:22). The only in-run text is "+1 HONEY" (game.js:184, 594). README:36 says "no explainer screens" is canon.
37. **nit: Escape quits to the title with no confirm. PARTLY WRONG.** Escape is Select (input.js:44), which in the hub opens the title menu (game.js:247). It saves first and only goes back to the menu, so nothing is lost. A confirm would still be nice.
38. **nit: Settings are minimal. CONFIRMED.** Only music, SFX and shake exist (ui.js:136; game.js:620-626).
39. **nit: Bottom-bar prompts overlap the CH label. CONFIRMED.** The CH label is at VH-14 (ui.js:117); the boon footer and "AN ECHO" label are drawn near the same line.
40. **nit: P1/P2 tags overlap when co-op players stack. CONFIRMED.** The tags are drawn with no separation (ui.js:107).
41. **nit: Pillars snag the player on the way to doors.** Same cause as #1 (game.js:129-131): there is no door-lane exclusion.
42. **nit: Camera drifts at walls. Design.** `camFollow` leans toward the keepers' centroid (models.js:233-238).
43. **nit: No locked feedback.** A locked keepsake only plays a low beep (game.js:296), which is inaudible in my build. There is no visual feedback.
44. **nit: Fountain heal amount isn't shown. CONFIRMED.** The Fountain heals 35% of max HP (game.js:185); the door never says so.
45. **nit: Typo in shop name. CONFIRMED.** "A ROOT-TOOTHS STALL" is at ui.js:188.

## New from the code (combat feel / controls / fairness)
55. **minor: Double-tapping the d-pad on a controller dashes.** The cartridge double-tap dash is enabled for pads whenever the d-pad is used (game.js:541): `dv.id.slice(0,2)!=='gp'||dv.dpad`. A d-pad player tapping twice to line up a shot dashes by accident, and burns a charge.
56. **minor: All four shoulders and triggers are dash.** input.js:61 maps buttons 4, 5, 6 and 7 to dash, so nothing is left for remapping or future verbs, and resting a finger on a trigger dashes.
57. **minor: Elites only get more HP.** Elites are 2.6× HP and 1.35× radius, with the same damage (game.js:136). Most of their threat comes from the extra wave and the bigger contact radius.
58. **minor: Super's freeze and long invulnerability make the player strobe.** The 0.6 s freeze (moves.js:51) plus 1.4 s of `inv` makes the post-super flicker the longest invisible stretch in the game (see #14).
59. **nit: Charging slows movement on every attack.** Every attack press sets `charging`, and holding A past 0.15 s cuts move speed to 55% (game.js:535; moves.js:100-101). Holding A through a combo feels sluggish.
60. **nit: Dash-strike window is short.** It's the 0.15 s dash plus a 0.16 s grace (game.js:419; moves.js:87). It works, but there's no input buffer before the dash ends.
61. **nit: Pickup magnet is tiny without a ZIP bud.** The radius is 48 without one, 88 with (game.js:587). Heart pickups only drop at 6% (game.js:390).
62. **nit: Spitter fire timing.** Spitters fire every 1.9 s at 125 range and 118 speed (game.js:470). Combined with #15, their shots are the main source of chip damage I didn't see coming.
