# ROOTDEEP 64: Mo's findings, checked against the code

Phase 2. I read `src/` and `README.md` after the blind write-up, which I left unedited. Line numbers refer to the source files in `src/`.

One note on how I tested, because it matters below. Each `pad`, `down` and `tap` driver command advances the game at least one frame. So every input I described as "on the same frame" in the blind notes actually landed one frame after the previous one. The code shows that this one-frame gap explains findings 22 and 23, and that truly simultaneous joins (finding 5) behave worse than what I saw.

Verdict tags: **CONFIRMED** (cause found), **DESIGN** (intended, per README or code comments), **PARTLY WRONG**, **WRONG**, **NOT FOUND**.

## A. The blind findings, mapped to code

1. **No drop-in mid-run (major). CONFIRMED.** `pollJoin()` is only called from `hubUpdate` (game.js:248) and `charsUpdate` (game.js:215). `runUpdate` (game.js:514) never calls it, and nothing queues a device for the next chamber. README:20-21 says joining works "in the Hearth or on CHOOSE YOUR KEEPER", so this is a design limit, but there's no on-screen feedback when someone tries.
2. **No clear way to leave (major). CONFIRMED.** The only drop path is `dropPlayer(p)` from B on keeper select (game.js:219). Nothing in the hub, pause (game.js:315-321) or menu (game.js:614-619) can drop a player, and P1 can never be dropped (`if(i<=0)return`, input.js:78).
3. **Dropping renumbers everyone (major). CONFIRMED.** `dropPlayer` splices `PL` and rewrites `ix` (input.js:78). Colour (`PCOL[p.ix]`, input.js:73), the HUD corner (`slots[i]`, ui.js:110-111), the tag, and the saved keeper choice (`RD.chars[p.ix]`, game.js:214/218/231) all follow the slot, not the person.
4. **Keyboard P1's back sends the whole party out, while other players' back drops only them (minor). CONFIRMED.** game.js:219: `if(p.ix===0){state='hub';return}dropPlayer(p)`. This is about seat, not device: a pad P1's B does the same as keyboard P1's X.
5. **5th device ignored silently; three joins on "one frame" (minor). CONFIRMED, and worse than observed.** There's no full-party message (input.js:75, 82). `pollJoin` returns after the first join each frame (input.js:82), so devices pressing A on exactly the same frame lose their press and must press again. My test only worked because the driver spread the presses over frames. The hub spawns each joiner at a random spot near centre (game.js:248), so stacking on existing players is expected.
6. **Second-keyboard seat not advertised (minor). PARTLY WRONG.** README:15 documents Keys 2 as arrows plus `,`. What actually happened is that my Z/WASD join was **Keys 1 (`kb1`) joining as P3** after a pad was P1 and Keys 2 had taken the arrows (input.js:42 and 44, `kb2On`). The hub footer mentions only `,` (ui.js:73), so the WASD+Z seat is still unadvertised in game.
7. **Join footer overlapped by the DESCEND label (nit). CONFIRMED, layout.** The footer is at `VH-14` (ui.js:73), and DESCEND is projected from the world at `DOOR` (ui.js:64). Nothing keeps them apart.
8. **"PAD n" numbering (nit). CONFIRMED.** The label is `'PAD '+(i+1)` from the browser's gamepad slot (input.js:57), so it's unrelated to player number.
9. **Joining praise. CONFIRMED.** First press becomes P1 (game.js:627-630), and the join banner is at game.js:248 and ui.js:75.
10. **Everyone is the same Will (major). CONFIRMED, and the honey unlock is effectively hidden.** `RDDEF.chars` defaults to 4×WILL (game.js:46). Unlocks are at game.js:208-209. Erin needs 3 honey gifts, but the gift (Start mid-talk, game.js:280) is only possible in the plain-talk branch. Whenever an NPC has a pending story beat (`talkEv`, game.js:269 and 277-278), the gift path is unreachable and the "GIFT HONEY" hint isn't drawn (ui.js:80-84 vs 84). Erin carried a "!" all night, so we could never gift. Rendering has no per-player tint: `drawCast(p.char, ...)` (models.js:266), and only the ring is coloured (models.js:263).
11. **Stacked players merge (major). CONFIRMED.** `movePlayer` collides with obstacles only, with no player-to-player separation (game.js:232-239). NPCs push each other (game.js:260-261) but not players.
12. **Players hidden under corner HUD panels (major). CONFIRMED.** Panels are fixed at the 4 corners (ui.js:110), and `camFollow` has no safe-area margin for them (models.js:233-244).
13. **Players hidden behind walls and props (major). CONFIRMED (no handling).** Players can stand anywhere in x 13 to W-13 (game.js:237), right next to the room walls and the stalagmite props at x≈10 / W-10 (models.js:316). There's no silhouette or occlusion pass anywhere in `src/` (I searched for silhouette, occlusion, x-ray and "behind").
14. **Damage numbers and barks pile onto P-tags (minor). CONFIRMED.** Tags (ui.js:106-107), floating texts (ui.js:108) and Will's "SELFIE!" (game.js:415) are all drawn near the same height with no layering or offset per player.
15. **Downed bodies carry no P-tag (minor). CONFIRMED.** ui.js:107 draws "STAND HERE" *instead of* the P-tag.
16. **Grubs and the boss cover player sprites (minor). DESIGN/NOT FOUND.** It's ordinary depth sorting. I found no outline for players under large enemies.
17. **No ring or tag for P1 in the hub when solo (minor). DESIGN.** The ring only draws when `PL.length>1||run` (models.js:263), and the tag only when `PL.length>1` (ui.js:66).
18. **Control banner over the arena (minor). CONFIRMED.** ui.js:118-119 draws it at `VH*.2` for the first 7 s of chamber 1, while foes are already live.
19. **Any controller drives any menu, including purchases (blocker for the shared wallet). CONFIRMED.** Every menu reads the merged `pressed()`/`nav()` (input.js:68-70): Mirror (game.js:285-288), Armory (289-293), Shelf (294-297), Shop (310-314), Pause (315-321). `RD.dew` is one shared wallet, and no ownership or "Pn bought" message exists. `pressed()` also reads devices that **haven't joined** (it loops over every `DEV`), so a spare pad, or `,` before Keys 2 has joined, can confirm menus too (input.js:48 and 68).
20. **Boon screen: one shared cursor (major). CONFIRMED.** game.js:308-309. There's a 0.35 s guard against accidental accepts, which is good, but no per-player vote or "Pn chose" line.
21. **Hints follow P1's device (major). CONFIRMED.** `lead0()` = `PL[0].dev` (ui.js:40) feeds `drawUI` (ui.js:134), talk (ui.js:80 and 84), sprouts (202), select footer (236), skip (256), death screen (266) and the control banner (118). Only the per-column keeper-select hint uses `p.dev` (ui.js:233), and so do the hub prompts (ui.js:71). That's why those two were correct.
22. **Two interacts on the "same frame": neither opens (major bug). CONFIRMED, cause different from what I thought.** P3's A opened the Codex (game.js:263-274). One frame later, my Z was read by `codexUpdate`, which **closes on A** (game.js:300). Board does the same (301), and Heat does too when locked (302). So one player's "open" press closes another player's freshly opened screen. In `hubUpdate`, only the first player in `PL` order with a press is served each frame (`return`, game.js:265-274).
23. **Simultaneous supers: only one fires (major bug). CONFIRMED.** `doSuper` refuses while `RUN.freeze>0` (moves.js:49). `runUpdate` returns early during the 0.6 s freeze (game.js:521), so every other player's super press in that window is dropped, not queued.
24. **Dialog freezes everyone (minor). CONFIRMED.** State goes to `'talk'` (game.js:273), which skips `hubUpdate` (game.js:640-641).
25. **Attack doubles as confirm; mashing skips results (minor). PARTLY WRONG.** The death screen does lock input for 0.8 s (game.js:654, ui.js:266), and the boon screen for 0.35 s (game.js:309). The problem is that A is still both "attack" and "confirm", so sustained mashing gets past these short lockouts.
26. **Pause: no "who paused"; Escape didn't close it (minor). PARTLY WRONG.** Any player's Start pauses (game.js:515), and the menu doesn't record who did. Keyboard Escape maps to `sel` (input.js:44), which the pause menu ignores (game.js:315-321), so it does nothing. My guess that Escape toggled pause was wrong: my earlier X press (`b`) resumed the game (game.js:315), which is why HP moved.
27. **The Echo note has no close prompt and ignores the first press (minor). PARTLY WRONG.** It does have a blinking "A: KEEP IT" prompt (ui.js:175), but only after 0.5 s, and in P1's device words. Input is ignored for the first 0.5 s (story.js:196). Gramps' A landed inside that window. It opens automatically on walk-over (game.js:519).
28. **No confirm on Mirror purchases (minor). CONFIRMED.** game.js:286-287.
29. **Doors go to the first player to touch (major). PARTLY WRONG / DESIGN.** In co-op a door needs a standing player to hold within 13 units for 0.6 s (game.js:602-604, README:44-45). That's an anti-accident measure, but it's still one player choosing for everyone, with no vote, no "everyone gather" and no on-screen hold meter. That's why brushing past a door does nothing and a stalled stick still triggers it.
30. **Shop heal only healed the buyer (minor). WRONG as stated.** BIG HEAL heals **every standing** keeper (game.js:190). Gramps was *down*, so he was skipped. It still spends the shared purse on nothing for a downed player, and it doesn't say so.
31. **No way to share health (minor). CONFIRMED / DESIGN.** Heart pickups drift to the *nearest* standing keeper, not the weakest (game.js:588 and 593).
32. **Door labels don't explain the reward (minor). CONFIRMED.** Labels are the god name or `d.kind.toUpperCase()` (ui.js:102). "DEW" is both a god (`GODS`) and the Moondew door kind (game.js:166 and 181). Fruit with no boon converts to petals (game.js:116-118).
33. **Honey unlock unclear (minor, really major for co-op). CONFIRMED.** See #10. "Three honeys" is a hard-coded line in a mission text (story.js:69), not the live count.
34. **Stats are party-only; codex vs wipe chambers (minor). PARTLY WRONG.** There are no per-player stats anywhere (`RUN.kills` is shared, game.js:381). The two chamber numbers measure different things: the Codex shows total chambers *cleared* (ui.js:150, `RD.stats.chambers++` on clear, game.js:158), and the wipe screen shows chamber *reached* (ui.js:260). It's a labelling issue, not a bug.
35. **Shared run rewards (praise). CONFIRMED.** Heart, fountain and boons are applied run-wide (game.js:183-185). Mirror ranks in `RD.mirror` apply to all keepers via `rdStats` (game.js:68-78).
36. **Revive UX (praise). CONFIRMED.** game.js:403 and 526-527, models.js:269-270, ui.js:107.
37. **Revive radius smaller than the drawn ring (minor). WRONG as stated; real cause found for the slow revive.** The radius is 20 (game.js:526), and the ring is drawn at scale 20 on a unit-radius mesh (models.js:18 and 270), so they match. Camera perspective fooled me. The 12 s revive happened because the reviver was being hit: contact knockback of 90 (game.js:461) pushes them outside the 20-unit radius, and progress decays at 0.6×/s while nobody is in range (game.js:528). The progress disc does exist (models.js:269) but is faint.

## B. Multiplayer code audit (beyond what I saw)

56. **major — A disconnected pad leaves a ghost player with stuck input.** When `getGamepads()[i]` becomes null, input.js:56 only sets `live=false`. `cur`, `mx` and `my` keep their last values, so a pad unplugged mid-walk keeps that player walking forever (`movePlayer` reads `p.dev.mx/my`, game.js:232). The player is never dropped or paused, and there's no "controller disconnected" message. On reconnect, the same index re-uses the same `DEV` object, so the seat recovers. That's good, but only by accident.
57. **major — Unjoined devices can drive every menu.** `pressed()`/`pressedBy()`/`nav()` loop over all `DEV`s (input.js:68-70), joined or not. Keys 2's `a` is bound to `,` even before Keys 2 joins (input.js:48). A spectator's pad can buy at the Mirror or pick a boon.
58. **major — Simultaneous joins drop presses.** `pollJoin` returns after one join per frame (input.js:80-83). A second device pressing A on the same frame doesn't join, and its A isn't cleared, so in `charsUpdate` it's simply lost. In the hub, the extra press also isn't consumed, and other systems that read `pressed('a')` can see it.
59. **minor — Two players on one keyboard are workable but lopsided.** Keys 1 loses the arrows and Right-Shift the moment Keys 2 joins (input.js:42-44). Keys 2 has no Select (`sel:false`, input.js:48). Its Start is Backspace. Browsers also cap simultaneous key rollover, so two players mashing on one board will drop keys. That's hardware, but worth a note.
60. **minor — Keeper choice is stored per slot.** `RD.chars[p.ix]` (game.js:214/218/231) means that after any drop, the player who inherits a slot also inherits that slot's saved keeper.
61. **minor — P1 is permanent and owns the "back to hub".** `dropPlayer` refuses `i<=0` (input.js:78). If P1 wants to leave, the whole session has to restart (quit to title keeps `PL`, game.js:321 and 627-631).
62. **minor — Revive logic.** It needs any non-downed teammate within 20 for 2.2 s, and decays 0.6×/s otherwise (game.js:526-528). Several revivers don't speed it up. Downed players can't crawl. `damagePlayer` only puts you down if someone else is standing; the last player to fall triggers Death Defiance, Phoenix or Collar before `rdDie` (game.js:401-407), as README intends. Edge case: a down caused by contact during `touchHit` returns early if `RUN` was nulled (game.js:461), which is fine.
63. **minor — Wipe while a teammate is mid-revive.** `rdDie` banks everything run-wide (game.js:196). No per-player tally exists, and the stats only add `coopRuns`/`coopBoss` (game.js:128 and 171).
64. **minor — Pause, Abandon and Quit are open to any player.** Any player's Start pauses (game.js:515), and any device can choose ABANDON RUN or QUIT TO TITLE (game.js:321). There's no confirmation and no "only the pauser can act".
65. **minor — The shop's B leaves for everyone.** Any device's B moves the whole party to the next chamber (game.js:314). Any A on the last row does the same (game.js:311).
66. **minor — Buds re-home to the lead when their owner goes down** (game.js:547). That's fine, but when an owner takes a hit, a random bud of theirs scatters (game.js:399). Pod pickup goes to whoever touches it (game.js:561-562), so it's first-come.
67. **minor — Camera framing with spread-out players.** `camFollow` widens fill up to 0.98 by spread (models.js:235-236), but the 4 corner panels (ui.js:110) and the top purse/boss bar (ui.js:113-121) sit inside that frame. With the boss bar up and 4 players, the safe area is noticeably smaller than what the camera assumes.
68. **nit — Hub spawn for a new joiner is random near the centre** (game.js:248), not beside the player whose device just joined.
69. **praise — Per-keeper state is cleanly separated.** HP, dash charges, cast ammo, combo, meter, cooldowns and char (game.js:120-121, moves.js:29-31). Foes target the nearest *standing* keeper (game.js:364). Keeper select is properly per-device (game.js:215-221). The co-op door hold (0.6 s) and boon accept delay (0.35 s) show the multiplayer edge cases were thought about.

## Summary

Cause found for every blind finding except #16 and #51 (art and depth). Wrong or partly wrong as stated: #6, #25, #26, #27, #29, #30, #34, #37, #45, #49. The biggest multiplayer problems in code are:
- the merged, any-device menu input (input.js:68-70), including devices that haven't joined
- hints that follow `lead0()` (ui.js:40)
- the camera ignoring downed players (models.js:312)
- slot-based renumbering on drop (input.js:78)
- no join path in `runUpdate`
- the stuck-input ghost after a pad disconnects (input.js:56)
- honey gifting being unreachable while an NPC has a story beat (game.js:269/277), which keeps everyone playing the same Will
