Grok Fable Astra
State of the Game

Since Version 14 the game has roughly doubled. It gained eighteen places and pocket worlds, a playable cast of nine with full movesets, a squad, a swinging grapple, item boxes, a wardrobe for NPC and a codex. It is now as much a collect-a-thon as a joke. It has still never been played by a person on real hardware, it still cannot be sold under its name, and its code is getting harder to change safely.

Sources: the live build (Version 31) and the Game repo (grok-fable-astra/ on claude/festive-hawking-dorqg0). Counts were read out of the running game by script; screenshots are from scripted runs, not hand play. Grades are judgment, not measurement.

Play

A−
Movement and platforming
▲ was B High priority

Double jump, a poop boost per jump on charges, a dash, a swinging grapple that only catches grapple points and ring tops, a ground slam, and a held Lag Spike that slows the world. 462 platforms. Mount Skill Issue has no checkpoints and was climbed end to end by a scripted climber; Swing-O-Rama was swung end to end.

Raise it: Nobody has played any of it by hand. Tune swing momentum, slam speed and the mountain gaps on a real pad.

B−
Combat
▲ was C+ High priority

Every playable character has their own blast, a special on Y and a move on B; twelve item-box power-ups; pit gates, reply-guy fights and the boss. Enemies still mostly walk at you.

Raise it: Give enemies two or three behaviours each, so the new kits have something to be clever against.

B+
Boss and endings
= B+ Medium priority

Unchanged since Version 14: the Algorithm, ROT x1000, the Last Grass, Meme Mountain, the First Post. Still the strongest single run of the game.

Raise it: A short scene where the cast you have played as shows up at the First Post.

A
Exploring and collecting
▲ was A− Low priority

33 named places plus four pocket worlds and a sky course. On top of the old 67s, drops, fragments and buddies: 16 rings to thread, 12 NPC looks, 12 NPC words, 60 item boxes, 74 codex entries, 99 checklist goals.

Raise it: A map screen with what is left in each place. The collection is now big enough that players will need it.

A−
Side content
▲ was B Medium priority

Nine gates in the pit, a coalition of runaway chairs, the Recount-O-Matic, David's loans and prophecies, ten podcast episodes and a personal audit, the giant debate, the livestream, the loosh pumps, the facility, the maze, the nuked patch, the pile-on.

Raise it: Tie two or three of these into the main story so they are not all optional detours.

A−
Playable cast and squad
new since v14 Medium priority

Nine characters unlock by finishing their quest and fetching their tower item. Pick four in the pause menu, swap with Z / D-pad right / 🎭. Each has a model, a passive, a blast, a special, a B move, and three have their own dash.

Raise it: Let each character say something when you swap to them, and give the squad a reason to swap mid-fight.

Raise it: Mark the lines tied to this year's memes so they are easy to swap later.

Lore conversations in each character's voice from the other games, Kael in his Guttercrown model, David as the Oracle on a gold throne, Bessie, Marc, the Hum, and all nine playable.

Raise it: One thread through the new places, for example the Hum and the facility leading into the Meme Mountain ending.

Parodies under new names: Bobby Screwdriver, Byron Pains, Big Eth, the Scroll Farm's loosh company, the facility. Funny and on-target about the attention economy and pile-ons.

Under the hood

D+
Code health
▼ was C− High priority

Thirty acts layered on each other and 121 wrapped-function chains. Three bugs today came from that: the mountain jump cap, grapple points placed 1,800 m up, and two name clashes.

Raise it: Stop and refactor before the next big feature: fold the wrap chains, split by act.

C
Testing
= C High priority

About twenty scripted play-throughs exist and caught most of today's bugs. They live in a scratchpad, not the repo.

Raise it: Move them into the repo with one command to run them.

Raise it: Work the release list below in order.

Content decisions made along the way

Real people

Three parodies under invented names: Bobby Screwdriver (a money-roast podcaster), Byron Pains (a giant debate podcaster) and Big Eth of the h7h7 Podcast (a cancel-culture pile-on). None uses real allegations or anyone's background; each satirises a format. They are still recognisable, which matters if this is sold.

David

He has his Guttercrown personality, look, resurrection contract, gold, aura loans and a giant throne, but no surname. The Guttercrown backstory (an expelled family, a stolen homeland, a puppet king) was left out because together with a Jewish-coded name it is an antisemitic caricature. The same recommendation applies to Guttercrown itself.

The Scroll Farm

Asked for with real nationalities running the bot farms; built with an invented company, LOOSH & CO., a subsidiary of the Algorithm, because real nationalities secretly harvesting people is a real-world conspiracy theory.

King Bibi and the title

Unchanged from Version 14: a real politician's nickname kept to podium-and-elections jokes, and a title that uses another company's product name.
