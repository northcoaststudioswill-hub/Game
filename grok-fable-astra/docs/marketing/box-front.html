Front cover idea

The cover is the title screen turned up: the hero nobody finished rendering, seen from behind, walking into Prompt Plaza with the three mascots waiting. The title is stacked word art in three clashing colours, one per mascot. Everything a real box would be embarrassed by is on purpose.

  • Art is a real frame from the game with the menus hidden. For print, re-render at 4× from the same camera. The hero re-rolls its look, so pick the frame you like and keep its seed.
  • The starburst carries the number that sells the ending: the rot goes to one million on Meme Mountain. It is the game’s own sticker, not a ratings mark; the real rating box goes where “Teens & up” is once the game is rated.
  • “Grok” is also the name of another company’s product. Clear the title, or pick a fallback, before anything is printed. The report covers this.
  • Alternate covers to test: the Algorithm’s pyramid over the checkerboard arena, or the Flow Pros van mid-air. Keep the same title block so the shelf reads as a series.
