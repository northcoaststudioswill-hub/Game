# Guttercrown: world notes

The in-game Codex (`js/lore.js`) is the source of truth. This file is the same material in one place for writers.

## The premise

**Highmarch** is the city above and the seat of the throne: white stone, clean water, a council that meets in daylight and votes as it is told. It grew rich on the
mines beneath it. When the seams ran dry it sealed the lower levels and stopped asking what became of the people
left inside.

**The Koheneim throne** rules the realm from Highmarch: the faerie elven queen **Eerin Koheneim** and her husband **Vilhelm van Koheneim IV**. Their grip is iron. Above ground there is a seal for everything, and breaking one means not being seen again. The Undercity is the one place where the seals are not asked for, and that is deliberate: every vice forbidden above is permitted below, so long as it stays below and the Deep Guild sends the Crown's tithe up the long stair. The shady quarter flourishes because the throne has decided that it may.

**The Undercity** is what was left: nine levels of dead workings, cisterns and forgotten foundations. No sun
reaches it, so its people plant what glows. No law reaches it either. What it has instead is the Ledger.

**The Deep Guild** was the miners' lending-house. When Highmarch walked away, the Guild held every debt in the
dark. It does not rule the Undercity so much as own it, one name at a time. It keeps its word exactly, which is the
most frightening thing about it.

**The Blood Ledger** is the Guild's book: every debt in the Undercity, and beside the worst of them a second
column, *fights owed*. A name leaves the Ledger three ways: pay in coin, die, or win the crown.

**The Blood Pit** is an old ore-sorting floor, eight-sided, ringed with braziers. The Guild found people will pay
to watch a debt collected. One on one, no surrender flag. The crowd decides how well you are remembered.

**The Guttercrown** was hammered from the brass of the first lamp the Guild ever lit down here. Whoever wears it
has their debt struck and takes a tenth of the gate. The Guild says it is only a prize. The Undercity treats its
wearer as the nearest thing to a monarch it has.

## You

A debtor. The Guild lent you a cave, a rusted blade and a sum you will never repay in coin. Tonight your name was
entered in the second column.

## Other faces

- **The Ledger-Keeper** (the Guildmaster in the street): pale hair, ink-stained fingers, remembers every sum.
  Polite, unhurried, without mercy, and will explain that this is not the same as cruelty.
- **The Fence**: "Everything fell off a cart. Different carts." The only person in the Undercity who has never
  owed the Guild a copper.

## The four trades

- **Steel (Fighters):** deserters, gate-guards, strikebreakers who learned to hold a line for someone else.
- **Shadow (Rogues):** every lamp the Guild lights makes a shadow, and somebody lives in it.
- **Flame (Sorcerers):** nobody teaches sorcery here. It turns up in a bloodline like a cough, and the damage goes on the family's debt.
- **Faith (Clerics):** the Light Beneath, a miners' creed that the sun did not abandon the deep places, it only went ahead to wait.

## Home

Guild housing: a stone room, a strip of cave, and rent quietly added to what you owe. Down here a lit lamp and a
bed of glowcaps are how you tell people you intend to stay alive. Glowcaps give a blue light you can read by.
Cave-bloom crystal grows a finger a year and hums when content. Moonwater is water that sat a season under
starlight crystal.

## The Tally

Down here a debt is a leash, and as your name grows people start owing *you*. They are your **Tallymen**:
the names in your own small ledger. They work off what they owe by doing what you would rather not be seen
doing, and they drink at the Low Lamp. One of them can stand in your corner as your **Second**.

## Mortal blows

Pit bouts are to the death, and the crowd expects a finish worth the gate. Fighters end it with the
**Headsman's Verdict**; rogues with the **Death of a Thousand Cuts**; sorcerers **Disintegrate**; clerics call
down a **Flame Strike**. A loser who is merely beaten is carried out and billed for the stretcher.

## Chits, schemes and wispdice

**Chits** are Mark's own paper: slips stamped with a long-eared rat. They are worth exactly what Mark says they are worth, at Mark's tables, and nothing anywhere else. He hands them out when one of his **schemes** goes wrong, which is often. A scheme is any plan Mark has that needs somebody else's coin. Half of them pay. Nobody, including Mark, knows which half.

**Wispdice** are Guild-work: dice made of captive lamplight that hang in the air over the table and cannot be loaded, shaved or palmed. The Guild made them to settle its own disputes. Mark has five, and has never explained how.

## The Koheneims

- **Queen Eerin Koheneim.** Faerie-born and elven, older than most family names. She is the law, and never raises her voice. The Ledger-Keeper's monthly account is said to be the only document she reads to the end. She allows the Guttercrown to exist because it amuses her that the cellar has a monarch too.
- **Vilhelm van Koheneim IV.** Her husband and the hand inside the iron glove. He commands the Seal-Wardens, signs the warrants, and sealed the lower levels himself. Rumour says he watches the champion's bout once a year from the Guild box, in a plain cloak.
- **The Iron Peace.** Forty years without a riot above, paid for by letting everything forbidden happen below.
- Names are spelled exactly as above: *Eerin Koheneim*, *Vilhelm van Koheneim IV*.
