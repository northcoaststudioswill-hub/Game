# GOT NEXT — who's who on the Block

Everyone already on the Block stays as they were: Coach Overtime, Sharpie, the Old Head, Soy Dog Sal, the fiends, the dice crew, the six ladder challengers, your homies. The people standing around without much going on now have habits: checking their phone, talking to each other, bobbing to the music, arms crossed, filming. On top of that there is one new character, NEXX, and seven regulars borrowed from the crew in your other games.

The borrowed regulars only say lines that already exist in those games (Pocket Lantern / Sprouts / Sproutwild / North Coast Knockout / Patch & Plate), quoted as written. Their names are hinted, not spelled out. NEXX's lines are new, written from your description, so read them and tell me what to change.

## New

- **Where:** leaning on his black car by the cage fence, west side of the Block. The plate says NEXX.
- **Who:** sick of the old basketball games ("same game every year with a new number on the box"), so he hangs out on the court instead. Goji berry wraps, Warzone, business ideas, makes his own clothes, works hard, drives everywhere, Discord demon, makes content.
- **What he does:** walk up and he turns and films you; otherwise he's on his phone, on a call, or bobbing. Talk to him for:
  - **RIDE:** he drives you to My Crib or The Cages ("I don't walk anywhere").
  - **CONTENT:** a daily challenge for your next game (2 dunks, 3 greens, an ankle-breaker, 2 threes, 4 assists or 2 blocks). Do it and he posts the clip: +200 HC, +60 Respect.
  - **GOJI WRAP:** free once a day. +3 Speed, +3 Acceleration, +5 Stamina next game.
  - **MERCH:** NEXX SUPPLY, sold only by him: Studio Hoodie (1,200 HC, +1 Handles, +1 Speed with Ball), Snapback (450), Runners (1,400, +1 Speed, +1 Acceleration).
  - **THE SERVER:** opens The Link (friends and crews).
