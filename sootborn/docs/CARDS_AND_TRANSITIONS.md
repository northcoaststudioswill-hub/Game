## 2. The rules for cards
- **Chapter card** (a named day or chapter): the NAME big, then its date. *THE LEDGER | Forty-three days after the furnace.*
- **Time card** (a jump): the date big, then place and one short line. *FOUR YEARS AFTER THE FURNACE | The Assay. Day three.*
- **Assay cards always say the day in the second line** ("The Assay. Day thirty-one."). The calendar strip reads that line, so it must stay.
- **Never** "later", "earlier", "from now", "ago" on a card. "THAT NIGHT", "MORNING" and "MEANWHILE" are fine: they are where you are, not how far you moved.
- **Prose cards** (a sentence instead of a date) are allowed only at the furnace count and at the very end.

## 3. Transitions
Each kind of jump has one look, used every time, so the player learns it once.

| Name | When | What you see and hear |
|---|---|---|
| **CUT** | Inside the same time | Hard cut to black, under half a second |
| **WAKE** | Back to the present below | White fades out, a valve hiss, Kael's breath; the day's card |
| **FURNACE** | Back to before the furnace | An orange bloom from the bottom of the frame, the heart sound; warm colour inside the scene |
| **STRIP** | To the Assay | The calendar strip slides in and runs to the day; a gold wash; the Assay card |
| **CHALK** | Same time, somewhere else | A chalk sun is drawn over the frame and wipes to the new place |
| **PAGE** | Into or out of a comic-book level | A page turns; panel borders close in, then open out |
| **ELEVEN** | Only three times: the opening, the furnace, the last flash | The count, one to eleven, cuts getting faster |
