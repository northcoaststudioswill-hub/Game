# SOOTBORN — story bible, additions v0.72 to v0.74
These sections were added to the story bible after v0.71. The project's "sootborn story bible" doc still holds the v0.71 text; the whole current bible is `docs/STORY_BIBLE.md` in the repo. Read this alongside it.

## The fourth ring, walked (v0.72)
- **What it is:** the clerks' ring of the Sun Spire. A white corridor with gilt doors, a teal lamp between each, tall windows, and an ochre line a hand wide painted on the floor from the service stair to every lamp and every door and nowhere else. The service walks on the line; a porter who steps off it loses a day of air. The porters repaint it themselves on rest days and buy the paint.
- **The head rule:** the Accredited do not turn their heads to whoever is speaking ("a voice is enough to know a rank by, so a face is a thing one does not spend"). Soot do. Kestrel Dray turns her head; nobody has cured her of it.
- **The bells:** one for a clerk, two for a House, three for a Marshal; for the High Auditor nobody rings anything. The service kneels at any of them. At two bells the clerks bow from the neck.
- **Who lives there:** Edric Sorrel (the last door on the length); Kestrel Dray, engineer, east manifold, from the Post on (a thumbprint of grease on her door handle; the riser beside her door is worn at the height of a hand); Maren Aldane, Second Clerk, whose lamp is always lit until the last visit. Sable keeps a stall of greens off Freight Three's cold crates: half sold to clerks, half back down the stair in nobody's book.
- **Soot words that give him away:** "Ta." "I" for "one". "Get up." Looking a porter in the face. A bare palm.
- **The three visits:** four years and a season after (new, watched); five years after (Kestrel at the pipe: "Four beats and a catch"; Tier Four's crates have not come up); six years after (colder: a porter kneels to him with no bell, because somebody above has looked at him; his gloves are laid out palms up on his desk; "She sent for me in the morning").
- **Flags:** `susp` (0 to 6, what the ring has noticed, carried between visits), `ring1` to `ring3`, `wrenPipe` (he put his hand on the riser with her).
- **Not decided by the owner, written to fill the place:** Maren on the fourth ring; the first porter moved to the kitchens; the gloves; the clerks' bow; who pays for the paint.

## Part Two looks ahead (v0.72)
- **The cold open:** nine years after, sunset, the Stair, Tavi on his left. It ends on somebody standing in front of the white door and "I knew the way he stood." It does not say who. (In the full level the door is held by the Auditor's Blade and then Oswin Hale.)
- **The book:** the Count, open, one line in red: "Brennick, K." Nobody has struck it; a gilt cuff holds a pen over it. Pays off in the Count (plan 16a).
- **Tavi at sixteen:** on the service stair with a bedroll and a satchel, chalking a sun on the Spire's wall. Lucan is the one who tells Kael the Spire knows about Tavi ("She says there is a child. Below, on your tier. One you brought up."). Pays off in "Tavi is coming up. Sixteen."

## Part Two's flashes, as built (v0.73)
- **Day thirty-one, after dark (the cloak).** He walks into the camp in Voss's cloak, with Voss's blood on the hem. Cadets of Violet and Teal get up as he passes and fall in behind him; nobody is called. Petra had "ruled the line" under his name. Oswin stands up for the first time in thirty-one days. The six lamps on the dais do not move: there is no line for a cadet in a Proctor's cloak. He breaks the white ribbon on the cache and gives it out by hand, in the order they are standing. "They did not follow me because I won. They followed me because I came back."
- **Behind the painted line.** A door behind the kitchens that no clerk uses, and a line painted across the floor. On the far side: the Spire's own valves (twelve wheels on that landing, the same foundry mark as Bank Seven), kept by people from Tier Six. They know who he is and have told nobody. A valve hand says "Brennick", very quietly. A lamp trimmer: "We hummed for her. Up here. They can't hear it through the stone." Flags: `sstair`, `svcValve` (he helped with the wheel).
- **Celestine's own Assay (fifty years before the furnace; she is twenty).** Decided by the owner: born on Tier Six, called Little Ember, taken up by an Accredited family, gilded, let them take the burns off her palms, gloves ever since; she won by shutting a valve on the other House. As built: that year the other House slept below, in the stack's undercroft, out of the storms, forty on one draught; the pump house wheel feeds their lamps and their air. She takes one glove off, palm flat on the pipe first. "It is only a valve. On Tier Six every child can turn one. Up here, nobody else would." Thirty-eight come up at first light; two do not wake. A Proctor writes "Genius." She says nothing. To Kael, six years after: "Whoever will turn the valve runs everything. I have run the Count that way for fifty years." She is seventy-six then, and seventy-nine at the last fight. Flag: `emberSeen`.
- **How he sees it:** in her room she takes off one glove and closes a weeping seat herself, the way only a valve hand would. His line after: "A valve hand. The High Auditor of the Array was a valve hand, and she had sat in a white chair and watched my wife walk into a furnace."
- **The barge (eight years after).** He sat a month and counted nothing. Aboard: Ilse, Sable, Hollis the welder ("He put a halberd butt in my arm once. For humming. I'd have liked to tell him it mended."), Bao the noodle seller at the wheel. Brand's halberd is lashed to the rail, butt down; nobody has said who brought it off the dock stair.
- **Seren and the line (three weeks before the furnace).** After last bell she puts a hand on Bank Seven: more goes in than comes out at the rack, a breath in every forty. Under the grating the main splits to a blind tee, capped, with an unnumbered wheel: a way round the meter built in when the bank was built, which everybody on Crew Three walks round. Orin: it fed the fever ward before the Count shut the fans; open it and forty beds breathe, and the audit shows a bled line with Crew Three's number on it; "They don't strike a line for that. They strike a crew." She leaves it shut. "Somebody will have to answer for it. Let it be somebody who knew." Kael, after: "She was not curious. She was choosing." Flag: `serenLine`.
- **Voice rule:** a flash in which you play somebody other than Kael is narrated from outside, in the present tense.

## The last chapter, as built (v0.74)
- **The book (nine years after, the night before the Stair).** Kestrel brings the Array board's own copy of the Count, which her engineers set the banks from. He carries days across it so that every tier is sent what it is owed; the Spire's rings give up what they were hoarding; Tier Four, struck and BALANCED with nothing, is written back in. The last red line is his own: "Brennick, K. Tier Six. Owed: nine years." He writes it in black. The banks turn to the new figures at first bell. (Where and how this happens is not the owner's; it was written to give the puzzle a place.)
- **Hollis** carries Brand's halberd up the Stair and grounds it at the top, butt down, the way a Warden does.
- **One for forty:** Celestine did the sum at twenty with two cadets who did not wake, and every day since. Seren did it too, the other way round. "They called me that too, before they gave me this name."
- **The last flash:** the heart-furnace and the count, to eleven, held.
- **Row K, if she is sent down:** Mara calls her "little ember" without knowing. She calls every new mouth on the row that.
- **Open question:** whether Tavi is his brother or his sister. The plan says brother; the written Part Two says "her"; the Codex says sibling.
