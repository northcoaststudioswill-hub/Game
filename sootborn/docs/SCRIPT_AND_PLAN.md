Written at v0.64 (2026-11-19) from a bot that played the whole story from New Game to THE END with nothing able to kill it (eight minutes, no errors). It jumped the four free-play stretches (the heist, Ashgate, First Fire, the lantern climb) and skipped through fights and levels; everything else ran as a player gets it.

## 1. The rule for time, from v0.64
- **One clock: the furnace.** Every card says how long before or after the furnace it is, or names a day of the Assay. No card says "from now", "later", "earlier" or "ago", because "now" keeps moving.
- **The four days below have names, not numbers:** Ashgate (41 days after), First Fire (42), The Ledger (43), The Way Out (160). "Day three" now only ever means the Assay.
- People may still say "nine years ago" in their own voice. Cards may not.

## 3. What is wrong with that order
- **The Assay plays as: day 40, night 1, day 19, nights 28–31, night 49, last day, day 3, night 25, day 26, the Pinning.** Day three comes after the last day. That is the real cause of "the timeline does not make sense"; the cards only made it worse.
- **Training is scattered:** the Table and the Drill on the first night, No Gloves on the second, after Assay fights that happen later than it.
- **The Proctor run interrupts the festival.** Four Assay pieces play between walking to the square and the ceremony starting.
- From Part Two on, everything already runs forward.

## 4. Proposed timeline (nothing is cut; pieces move)
Keep the Valley as the cold open (the one deliberate jump: what he becomes). Keep one flash inside each day so the slow days still have a fight in them. After that, **time only ever moves forward inside each thread.**

| Where | Now | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open | The Valley (day 40) | The Valley (day 40) |
| Before the title | The Pairing (night 1) | The Pairing (night 1) |
| Ashgate, that night | Table, Drill, **Ford** | Table, Drill, **No Gloves** ("three years of that"), then **the Camp** (day 3) |
| First Fire, before the ceremony | Lamp, Dais, Proctor, On the Ground | **The Ford** (day 19) only |
| First Fire, that night | No Gloves, Last Night, Keep | **The Island** (night 25), **the Standard** (day 26) |
| The Ledger, that night | Camp, Island, Standard | **The Lamp** (28), **the Dais** (30), **the Proctor** and **On the Ground** (31) |
| The Way Out, before he leaves | (nothing) | **The Last Night** (49), **the Keep** (last day) |
| After END OF PART ONE | The Pinning | The Pinning |

- Result: made (½y, 2y, 2½y) → Assay day 3 → 19 → 25 → 26 → 28 → 30 → 31 → [40, already seen] → 49 → last day → the Pinning → the Spire.
- A stricter version (all training on night one, the Assay's first half on night two, its second half on night three, nothing mid-day) is possible, but it takes the fights out of the daytime and makes nights two and three very long. I do not recommend it.
- **Cost: medium-low.** The pieces are already separate; it is moving them, re-pointing what each hands back to, adjusting about a dozen lines that refer to what he has "just" dreamed, and re-running the whole-story bot. One round.
- **Needs your yes before I move anything.**
