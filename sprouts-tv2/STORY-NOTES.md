## Lore proposed here

1. **Who called them.** The North Light is the light that calls keepers. It had gone dark, so its last flame, the
   Lanternwisp, walked the roads and called them herself. Erin saw a pale light on the road the night before she
   came; Marcus saw one in his mirrors; Will followed one on his bike. Scout works this out in her notebook. This
   answers Will's open question from Nov 17. It is the biggest thing proposed here.
2. **The leak job.** The call Flow Pros were on ("it's leaking", no what, no where) was the North Light's oil line.
   Nobody left a name. "Just a light on the line."
3. **Ranger is local.** His grandmother set the four trail posts, one for every road a keeper might come in by.
   Nobody came for forty years. He kept them standing.
4. **Scout is local too**, and writes everything down because the valley forgot who lit the Lantern.
5. **Erin is not local.** She was called here like Will. Lines that made her sound like she grew up in the valley
   were given to Ranger.
6. **The North Light rests.** Three nights after it is answered it banks down to an ember: "It rests once it's been
   answered." It is not out. This is why it can be dark in Sproutwild without Sproutwild having to explain it.
7. **The skip** was there before the crack. Thornback half remembers at the midpoint ("Keep... and a second word. I
   have lost it") and fully at the call ("One to keep. One to call").
8. **The wedding** is planned and not held. The book ends on a date left open: "when the valley can spare us".
9. **Expedition postcards** point outward without naming anything: a town with no lantern, the foot of the mountain,
   a far meadow, the Crooked River under another name.

## The story in order

The crack. Will arrives last; Erin fills him in. Ranger, Scout, and Marcus with Derek at the van (day one). Marks at
the first shard, more at two, Thornback at the last. The blaze: whole, and it still skips (midpoint). A night passes.
The call. The coast. The pier. The flooded lamp room. Marcus works out it was the job. Ten sprouts and two plumbers.
The lens home. Nightfall: the relight, then everyone at the Lantern (the ending), then Thornback's third thing under
the mountain. Three nights later the North Light rests.
